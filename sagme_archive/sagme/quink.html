Quink

Date: 2026-07-17 17:22 | Tags: puzzles, old games

Quink is a 1980 puzzle/game by Stephen Sniderman, published at GAMES #16.

Here is the board and rules as shown in the magazine:


This is essentially a Solitaire-like puzzle. But with two boards, both with the same setup, it becomes a race between two players to first achieve the five pentominoes that guarantee a victory. This idea, of course, can be applied to other Solitaire puzzles.
