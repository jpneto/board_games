Trox

Date: 2028-03-03 13:45 | Tags: boardless, flip pieces, new games, tile games

Trox is a 1997 game by Peter Andrew, published at Ceméa and Trox Games.

The game does not have a board, but players create a play arena play placing trapezoid tiles like these

There are twelve tiles with different color combinations (but always in black/red/yellow).

Each player has six pieces (two red, two black, two yellow). 

Rules:

  • Initially, the first player:
    • drops a tile
    • drops a friendly piece on the triangle with the same color
  • Then, on his turn, the player either:
    • drops a tile that must connect with a tile already on board (the two tiles sharing a triangle edge), and drops a friendly piece on this new tile [not sure if the same-color rule still applies]
    • moves a piece to any occupied triangle (not necessarily adjacent)
  • A piece surrounded by three enemy pieces is neutralized, i.e., it flips to the other side (which have no markings) and no longer moves
  • Pie rule: the second player can decide to switch places before his second turn
  • Wins the player that makes a big triangle, made of four tile triangles, occupied by friendly pieces
    • neutral pieces can also be used in this winning shape, except a neutral piece cannot be at the big triangle's center
