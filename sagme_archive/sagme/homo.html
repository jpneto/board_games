Homo

Date: 2030-01-11 21:00 | Tags: older games

Homo is a c.1898 game by Henry J. Weintz, published at De La Rue.

The game is to be played on the standard 8x8 checkered board of Chess/Checkers.

Each player has four round pieces, four square pieces, and four triangle pieces,

the board setup

Rules

  • On his turn, the player moves a friendly piece
    • rounds move to any orthogonal or diagonal adjacent square
    • squares move to any orthogonal adjacent square
    • triangles to any diagonal adjacent square 
  • Captures are custodian, ie, if the moving piece is placed and it plus another friendly piece of the same shape, have between them an enemy piece, that enemy piece is captured
    • after each capture, the player moves again
  • A player loses the game either by (a) being left without a pair of similar shapes, or (b) being stalemated
    • One player at the disadvantage may claim for a draw: then the other player has 30 moves to try to win. If he is unable, the game ends in a draw
