Entrapment

Date: 2027-09-10 16:29 | Tags: wall pieces, new games

Entrapment is a 1999 game by Rich Gowell, published at Gowell Classic Games.

The game is played on a 7x7 grid, where walls can be placed between squares.

Each player has three pieces (the roamers) and 25 walls with their color. A wall placed horizontally is resting, and a wall placed vertically is standing.

Rules:

  • Initially, players take turns dropping each one of their roamers in empty squares
  • On her move the player:
    • first, move a friendly roamer by either:
      • move once or twice to orthogonally adjacent empty squares on a straight line
      • jumping over a friendly roamer landing on the immediate next square that must be empty (and wall free)
      • jumping over a friendly resting wall (which becomes a standing wall) landing on the immediate next square that must be empty
    • second, move a friendly roamer (using the previous rules) or place a new resting wall
      • if all walls are already on board, the player can move a resting wall to a new position
  • To balance the game, on her first move, the first player just makes one action, either moves a roamer or places a resting wall
  • Entrapment: if a roamer is surrounded and cannot move, it is entrapped. If its player cannot do an action to provide its liberty in the next turn, the entrapped roamer is captured (if there is an option, the player is forced to take it)
    • If a friendly roamer is moved in order to release the entrapped piece, and thus become entrapped itself, it is immediately captured
    • If two or more roamers become entrapped, the player must choose and capture only one of them
  • Wins the player that captures all enemy roamers

For more information the official rules at BGG. There's also a ZRF to play the game, and the game can also be played with AiAi.
