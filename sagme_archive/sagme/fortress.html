Fortress

Date: 2030-08-16 15:54 | Tags: old games

Fortress is a 1983 uncredited video-game, published by SSI.

The game is played on a 6x6 board, initially empty.

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player either:
    • places a new castle (of level one) on an empty square not controlled by the adversary
    • upgrades a friendly castle (up to level three) 
  • Each castle on level N provides influence of strength N to the orthogonal adjacent squares
    • Each square is controlled by the player with the highest influence
  • If the square of a player's castle becomes controlled by the adversary, the castle is captured
  • After 21 turns the game ends, and wins the player controlling more squares
