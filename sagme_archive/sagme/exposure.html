Exposure

Date: 2030-08-02 14:48 | Tags: new games

Exposure is a 2003 uncredited game, published at Skye Marketing Corporation.

The board is played in the following board, with a random setup:

one possible setup

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player takes all pieces, of a selected color, which are at the top of their respective columns.
  • When the board is empty, wins the player with more pieces.

For example, given the previous setup, the first player could start by removing two yellow stones. Then, the second player could reply by removing the two green stones (one of them just became the top of its column, after the yellow stone was removed).

This is a purely tactical game, where a good 'middle-game' planning will decide the game.
