Cavendish

Date: 2027-05-14 12:12 | Tags: old games, moku

Cavendish is a 1986 game by Maureen Hiron, published at  Great American Trading Company.

The game is played on the intersections of this board,

Each player has 35 pieces.

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player either: 
    • drops a friendly pieces on an empty intersection
    • with an already friendly piece on board, jumps over two adjacent enemy pieces in sequence, landing in the immediate next intersection (that must be empty); the jumped pieces are captured
  • On her second turn, the first player must drop a piece not within three intersections of her first. 
  • Wins the player that makes a 5 in-a-row

The original rules:

This is a kind of Pente but in hexagonal boards, and replacing custodian capturing with jumps over pairs of pieces.
