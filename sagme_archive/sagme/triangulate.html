Triangulate

Date: 2029-02-02 15:33 | Tags: new games, stack pieces

Triangulate is a 2001 game by Allan Bedford.

The game is played on a 8x8 board.


initial setup
(large pieces will be represented as blue/red pieces)

Each player has eight small stackable pieces and 32 large pieces.

Adjacency is considered orthogonally and diagonally.

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player selects a friendly small piece and either:
    • moves it to an adjacent empty square, placing a friendly large piece under it
    • moves it to an adjacent square with a long enemy piece, then the player replaces it with a friendly large piece
    • moves on top of an adjacent small piece (of either color)
      • the small pieces that are under others, cannot be moved
      • stacks of small pieces cannot have more than two pieces, except for the winning combination (see below)
    • moves over any number of connected unoccupied large pieces of his color, eventually ending on top of another small piece (of either color); jumps are not allowed
  • On her first turn, the second player places a friendly large piece on an empty square not adjacent to a friendly piece
  • Wins the player that makes a stack of three small friendly pieces
