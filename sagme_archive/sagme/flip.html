Flip

Date: 2030-01-18 09:21 | Tags: flip pieces, checkers, old games

Flip is a 1976 uncredited game, published by Number Jack Enterprises.

The game is played on an 8x8 board:


the game's initial setup

There are 24 pieces with two-color sides. Each player gets twelve and place them on their first two rows with their color faced up.

Rules

  • On her turn, the player either:
    • moves a friendly piece to one (orthogonal or diagonal) adjacent empty square
    • jumps over an adjacent enemy piece landing on the immediate next square (that must be empty)
      • the jumped piece is flipped
      • jumps can be multiple
  • Wins the player that first place twelve friendly pieces in the adversary initial positions

Like most Halma-like games there is a drawish strategy of leaving pieces on their initial positions. It is suggested, if this is an issue, to add the following rule: if the twelve adversary positions are all occupied, having at least one friendly piece there, the player wins.
