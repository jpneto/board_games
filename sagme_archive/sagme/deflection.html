Deflection

Date: 2030-11-22 16:58 | Tags: old games, rotating pieces

Deflection is a 1989 game by Dave Taylor, published at Jupiter Corporation.

The game is played on the following board,


initial setup

Each player has six discs and six reflectors. 

Rules

  • On his turn, the player either:
    • moves and/or rotates one or two deflectors
      • deflectors move like Chess Queens (so, no jumps allowed) 
      • deflectors move to empty positions 
    • moves a disc by bouncing over friendly deflectors
      • discs bounce in and out of a deflector over lines not covered by the deflector (again, no jumps allowed)
      • discs must change direction at each new bounce
      • if a disk lands on an enemy disk, this one is captured by replacement  
  •  In the first turn, the first player can only more/rotate one deflector (to compensate for first-player advantage)
  • Wins the player that captures all enemy disks
