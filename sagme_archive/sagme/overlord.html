Overlord

Date: 2028-06-09 07:30 | Tags: old games, stack pieces

Overlord is a 1974 uncredited game, published at Condor.

The game is played on a 9x7 board, having a wall at the middle row,


the initial setup is to place the pieces
in the first two rows of the board

Each player has 14 stackable pieces.

The game comes with poorly written rules, making it very hard to understand how stacks work and how they move.


rule summary at the game's back
(didn't find a readable original ruleset)

Fortunately, Tom Bell, in 2012, made an effort to decode the rules, and reinterpreted them to make Overlord a playable game. You can read them here.
