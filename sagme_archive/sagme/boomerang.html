Boomerang

Date: 2027-06-04 14:54 | Tags: old games

Boomerang is a 1976 game by Peter Pallat, published by Otto Maier Verlag.

Each player has 10 pieces off board (there are five colors, up to five players), three of them in a special reserve.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player either:
    • drops a friendly piece (not from the special reserve) on an empty position
    • moves a friendly piece, already on board, to an adjacent position
  • If a piece moves to a position occupied by an enemy piece, that piece is captured
    • however a capture is only valid if done in the direction shown by the arrows (the orange edges don't have arrows, and cannot be used for capturing purposes)
    • if there are one or two extra enemy pieces in the immediate positions, in the direction of the arrow, those pieces are also captured
    • captured pieces are placed back in the opponent's special reserve
    • capture is mandatory
  • A player that occupies three positions cornering an orange triangle is able to, in that same turn, drop a piece from his special reserve.
  • The player with less than three pieces on board loses the game
