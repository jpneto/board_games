Porsta

Date: 2030-09-27 09:42 | Tags: older games

Porsta is a c.1940 uncredited game, published by Porst-Spiel.

There are pieces of six colors with 24 pieces per color. Each player takes three colors.

Rules 

  • On her turn, the player places a friendly piece on an empty circle
  • Wins the player with the largest connected group of friendly stones of the same color

The ruleset also considers placing the stones in a color cycle, or taking them from a bag. 

The game came also with a box for players to throw pieces and try to place them in the correct compartment, making it a dexterity game.
