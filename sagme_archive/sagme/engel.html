Engel

Date: 2029-05-18 17:23 | Tags: old games, pencil & paper

Engel is a 1975 game by D. Engel.

This is a pencil and paper game. The game is played on a 5x5 board.

Rules:

  • Players take turns drawing a segment from the center of one square to the center of another square located in the same row or column
    • a new segment cannot cross an existing line or close a loop
    • a segment can start/end at the endpoint of an already drawn segment
  • Wins the player that stalemates the adversary

an ongoing match;
despite using here two colors, this is an impartial game
