Plasm

Date: 2027-04-30 10:04 | Tags: old games, pencil & paper

Plasm is a 1974 uncredited game, published by 3M.

The board is an unusual one, composed by a partial tiling of pentagons:

This is a pencil and paper game, so players can just print the board and mark their positions.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player marks an empty pentagon as his own (say, using X and O marks) adjacent to any friendly mark (except for their first move)
    • If the player does not have any valid adjacent positions to mark, then he can continue on any empty position on the board
  • When the board is full, wins the player with highest score (there's a total of 101 points)
    • The player that finishes surrounding a star scores 5 points, a half star scores 3 points, and a diamond scores 2 points
