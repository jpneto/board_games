Rodin

Date: 2027-05-10 14:11 | Tags: old games, checkers

Rodin, or Rodan's Fountain, is an uncredited undated game published by Nikko.

The is an elimination game of the Checkers family (but without promotions) with jumps more similar to Halma.

  • Setup. The game starts with the twenty pieces placed on the twenty dotted sections of the first three rows. Seven pieces on the first and third row, six pieces on the second row.The heavy lines are the players' "home field".
  • Players can move their men from intersection to intersection one man at a time, moving forwards, backwards or sideways to the right or left. However, any of a player's men that are on a heavy line in the player's "home field" may only be moved if the movement is forward. It may also jump, but the jump, as in a move, must be forward. Once the player's man is clear of the "home field", it may move freely forward, backwards or sideways as mentioned above.
  • A player can move his men by jumping over his own and/or his opponent's men. It is possible to jump over any number of his own or his opponent's men during a turn but the jumps must be continuous and never over more than one man at a time. When a player has jumped his man over one or more of his opponent's men, the opponent's men jumped are removed from the game.
  • Goal. When one player has only two of his pieces left on the board, the other player is the winner.
