Diagares

Date: 2029-07-20 15:47 | Tags: old games, pencil & paper

Diagares is a uncredited game published in 1984 at David Pritchard's Five Minutes Games (quite possible, the game was designed by Pritchard himself).

The game is a pencil and paper game, where players score points for each achieved pattern:

The game is asymmetric, and very likely quite unbalanced, so two matches must be played where players switch sides.
