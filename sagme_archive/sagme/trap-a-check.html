Trap-A-Check

Date: 2027-02-26 13:15 | Tags: old games

Trap-A-Check is a 1944 uncredited game, published by Toy Creations.

The game is played in this board,

the initial setup

Each player has eight ring-like pieces that start on the board, as shown.

Rules

  • On his turn, the player moves a friendly piece to an adjacent empty circle
  • Any group, of one or more connected pieces, not adjacent to an empty circle (i.e., they have no liberties à lá Go) are captured and removed from the board
  • Wins the player that captures all enemy pieces

I'm not sure how is it possible for such a small army, that needs to move near enemy groups, to be able to capture anything without the help of the adversary. The game seems to have a fatal design flaw.
