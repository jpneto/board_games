Buku

Date: 2029-01-12 13:29 | Tags: sowing, new games

Buku is a 2001 game by Jorge Gomez Arrausi.

The game is played on a checkered 8x8 board, where each square has one piece,

There are 65 pieces, 64 start on board and one extra piece is given to the second player. Each player owns a square color (typically Black and White or, in like the previous diagram, Red and Blue).

Rules:

  • One player controls the board rows, the other the board columns, let's denote both as lines.
  • On his turn, the player chooses one of his lines, picking all the stones placed there.
    • then, the player selects any square and starting sowing those pieces, one per orthogonally adjacent square; the player can change direction to the left or right, as many times as he likes (but cannot sow twice in the same square)
    • all sowed squares with three or four pieces are captured 
  • If a player repeats a position, all pieces on the board are given to the opponent [probably it would be cleaner just enforcing a Ko rule]
  • When the board is made entirely of squares with zero or one pieces, the game ends
    • each player gets the pieces that are on squares of his color
    • wins the player with more pieces
