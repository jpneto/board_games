Attacco

Date: 2026-09-04 09:15 | Tags: older games

Attacco is a c.1900 game by George Howard Monks (the inventor of Halma), published by Adolf Sala.

It is played in a 11x17 board with the following markers,

Each player has 22 pieces that start at the dotted squares. The filled square is the player's stronghold.

Rules:

  • On his turn, a player selects one friendly piece and either:
    • moves to an adjacent light-colored square (orthogonally or diagonally)
    • jumps over any piece landing on the immediate next square, that must be empty and light-colored
      • if the jump is over an opponent piece, this piece is captured
      • jumps can be multiple but are not mandatory, and the player may stop jumping whenever he wants
  • Dark squares are walls that cannot be moved in or jumped over
  • Wins the player that moves a piece into the opponent's stronghold, or captures all enemy pieces

There is (possibly less interesting) variant of the game [1], that only allows jumps over enemy pieces.
