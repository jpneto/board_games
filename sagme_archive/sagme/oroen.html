Oroen

Date: 2028-11-10 08:10 | Tags: new games, connection, tile games

Oroen is a 1999 game by Corné van Moorsel, published at Cwali.

The game is played in a 10x6 board placed vertically (thus under the force of gravity),

The game comes with the following tiles (one set per player):

  • four pieces 1x2
  • three pieces 1x3
  • two pieces  1x4
  • one piece 1x5

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player places vertically one friendly piece in a board column; the piece will rest down (as under the force of gravity)
  • There are two restrictions to place a tile
    • a) the top edge of a piece cannot be at the same height as the top edge of an adjacent piece of either color (except when pieces touch the top edge of the board)
    • b) it is illegal to leave just one unit square empty in the playing column
    • if it is not possible to satisfy both restrictions, b) takes precedence over a)
  • Wins the player that either:
    • makes the last move, i.e., the adversary was unable to satisfy either restrictions a) or b)
    • makes a path of unit squares of his color connecting two opposite edges
