Arène

Date: 2028-01-21 14:57 | Tags: new games

Arène is a 1997 board by Stéphanie Czinober and Stéphane Bayle, published at La Mèche Rebelle.

The game is played in the following board,

notice that there are three steps going down to the middle arena.

Each player has six pieces which are placed initially like this:

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player moves two friendly pieces, each to an adjacent empty position
    • pieces can step down the steps, but cannot step up
  • A piece with no liberties is captured, but captures are only possible in the middle area
    • the upper levels do not provide liberties for the arena's positions, otherwise pieces at those edges could be made uncapturable
  • A player wins by capturing two enemy pieces

Given the board topology, the maximum liberty a position can have is three (at the arena's edge the available liberties are just two), so it is quite easy to surround pieces.
