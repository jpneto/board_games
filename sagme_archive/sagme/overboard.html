Overboard

Date: 2026-11-06 17:31 | Tags: old games

Overboard is a 1977 game by Alex Randolph, published at Lakeside. 

The game is played on the intersections of a 7x7 board, and each player has 18 pieces. All pieces start on the board in one of four allowed patterns (cf. official rules below).


Rules:

  • Each player, on his turn, either:
    • slides one friendly piece, as long as he wants, over a row and column
      • this slide will push other pieces along the chosen direction (even if there are empty spaces in between)
      • it is necessary that the slide will push one or more enemy pieces off the board
      • it is illegal to push off friendly pieces
    • moves a friendly piece to an adjacent empty square
  • Wins the player that pushes off all enemy pieces
The proposed goal seems quite drawish. It's easy to simply move back and forward, if the opponent does not have enough pieces to surround the player. Perhaps a rule like 'last player to move, wins' would be better (that would mean removing the option of simply moving a piece, or at least, restrict it more).
