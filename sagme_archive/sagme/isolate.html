Isolate

Date: 2028-12-22 09:52 | Tags: new games

Isolate, aka Zeparate, is a 2003 game by Guido Lap, published at Educational Insights.

The game is played on a 7x7 board

There are 16 pieces, four pieces per each one of four colors. For two players, each player picks two colors.

In the following rules adjacency is only vertical or horizontal.

Rules:

  • Initially, players take turns placing one friendly piece on an empty square adjacent to another piece already on board (except for the first piece)
  • On her turn, the player slides vertically or horizontally a line of two or more pieces, over one or more empty squares
    • Ko rule: a player cannot undo the previous adversary movement
    • pieces cannot be pushed over the board edge
  • When the pieces are separated in two or more unconnected groups, the smaller group is removed 
    • if the smallest groups are of the same size, the player decides which one to remove
    • a group that contains any piece that just moved, cannot be removed
      • so, if all removable groups have moving pieces, the player is not able to remove any
    • the removed pieces are given to their respective players 
    • whenever a player is able to remove a group, she moves again
  •  Wins the player that gets four pieces of one of her colors
    • if both players get their four colors, the player that just moved loses
