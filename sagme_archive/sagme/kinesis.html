Kinesis

Date: 2027-10-15 08:15 | Tags: old games, tile games

Kinesis is a 1991 game by Adam Byer, published at Cherry Stree Games.

The game is played on a 20x10 board,


the initial setup

Each player has eight pieces, and there are 24 shared domino pieces.

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player first makes a movement, then a slide.
  • Movement:
    • Move a piece
      • A piece can move one space forward, backward, up, or down, but not diagonally.
      • The destination space must be either empty or occupied by a free domino (a domino with no pieces) or a domino with a single piece, regardless of its owner.
      • A domino can hold a maximum of two pieces.
    • Move a domino
      • A player can move any domino that holds at least one of their own pieces
      • The domino can move in a straight line to an empty space in any orthogonal or diagonal direction [I assume it cannot move over other dominoes]
      • The orientation of the domino must remain unchanged
      • A moving domino may pass through a space occupied by another piece, regardless of color, but in doing so, it must "pick up" that piece. 
      • To pick up a piece, the domino must start its movement with only one piece on it.
  • Slide:
    • A piece can slide across one or more adjacent dominoes before descending back onto the board.
    • A piece cannot descend at the marked black squares
  • The first player to reach the opponent’s starting column with one of their pieces wins the game.  

The page at the Jeux Strategie website has several examples of moves and slides.
