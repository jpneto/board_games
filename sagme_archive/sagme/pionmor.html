Pionmor

Date: 2027-07-23 13:30 | Tags: wall pieces, incomplete rules, new games, moku

Pionmor is a 2000 game by Bernard Tavitian (of Blokus fame), published online.

I cound not find the original rules. The game won Dé d’or 2000 in the Boulogne-Billancourt international game design contest and, as most of the submitted games, are not available online.

However, there was a simplified version of this game that was launched, in 2004, as a Palm M500 game (software by Denys Seguret).

The rules of this variant played on a 9x9 board:

  • On his turn, the player drops one friendly piece on an empty square, and then places a wall between two squares
    • Any enemy piece surrounded by walls is removed from the board, and the player scores 1 points
    • Any 5 in-a-row, the player scores 5 points
  • Wins the player with more points.

Regarding the board, it seems the inner 3x3 squares are also made by walls.
