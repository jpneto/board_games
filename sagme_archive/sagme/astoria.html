Astoria

Date: 2027-11-19 16:18 | Tags: new games

Astoria is a 1992 game by Tom Boutell, published online in the old usenet group rec.games.abstract.

The game is played on an even-sized square board (say 8x8). Each player has enough pieces to cover half the board (half of them are triangles, half are circles).

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player either drops a friendly circle or triangle on an empty square
    • The triangle must point to north, south, east or west
  • After the piece placement, the player decides an orthogonal direction and pushes all pieces of a row (if east/west) or column (if north/south) one step following that direction
    • Any piece at the edge is pushed off board and captured
    • This is only valid for rows/columns where the player has a higher score than the adversary
    • To compute the score of a row/column, account for the friendly pieces in it: 
      • each circles is worth 1 point, 
      • each triangle pointed at the chosen directions is worth 3 points
      • the remaining triangles do not add to the score
  • The game ends when all initial pieces are dropped: wins the player with more pieces on board (of either shape)
