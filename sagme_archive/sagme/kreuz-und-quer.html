Kreuz und quer. A board game for 2–4 people – exciting and entertaining.

My dear game friends! This game is easy to learn and shows an unusual game progression. The aim is to reach the opponent's goal with your pieces and at the same time to prevent their progress. Whoever manages this first is the winner.

Included with the game: The game board and 4×4 colored playing pieces. At the beginning of the game, a game leader is chosen, who is responsible for dividing the 16 game pieces as follows: they are mixed – with the back facing up – and randomly assigned to the players. You may draw as long as you like, until each player has four pieces. The back of the pieces shows the color red, yellow, green, or blue. Only when all four players have their pieces do they turn them over to reveal their colors. This way, no one knows in advance which colors they have. The goal of the game is to move your own colored pieces across the board to the opposite side — that is, into the goal area of the same color.

How is the game played? In turns, each player moves one of their pieces forward along a chosen path (either vertically or horizontally). Diagonal movement is not allowed. Therefore, you must choose the path carefully. A piece may not move onto a space that is already occupied. You must also consider that your path may be blocked by other players. Even when it seems possible to move a piece freely, you must consider whether the chosen path is correct and free of obstacles. Strategic planning is essential!

If you are lucky, you will reach the goal quickly. However, as you must bring all your pieces to their color-coded goal spaces, good planning is key. The first player to achieve this wins the game. In the case of a tie, the player who reached the goal first with a majority of pieces wins. To make the game even more fun and fair, the game master may act as a referee. Have fun and good entertainment!
