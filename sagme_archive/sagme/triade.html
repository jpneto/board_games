Triade

Date: 2030-05-03 15:18 | Tags: new games

Triade is a 1992 game by Reiner Knizia, published at Spielbox magazine issue 1992 #2.

The game is played on a hexhex board of size 4:

Each player has 31 pieces that start on each player's reserve.

Rules

  • On her turn, the player drops a friendly piece on an empty hex
    • the player may continue to drop pieces on empty hexes that are neighbors to three or more of their own pieces
  • The game ends when one player drops all her pieces
    • The winner gets a score equals to the number of pieces in the adversary's reserve
