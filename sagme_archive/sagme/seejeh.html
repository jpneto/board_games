Seejeh

Date: 2027-08-13 07:12 | Tags: old games

Seejeh is a 1975 game by Judith Shepard Rosenfeld, published at Klee.

The game is played on a 7x7 board, initially empty:

Each player has 24 pieces.

Rules:

  • Initially each player, on his turn, drops two friendly pieces on empty squares -- except at the center -- until all 48 board squares are occupied
    • the first player must have at least one piece adjacent to the central square
  • Then, on his turn, the player moves a friendly piece to an orthogonally adjacent empty square
    • Captures are custodian: an enemy piece sandwiched by the moving piece, and another friend, is captured
    • After a capture, the player may continue to move if there are other captures available
    • Captures are not mandatory
  • Blockade rule: 
    • If a player is unable to move, he may invoke blockade. This means the player can remove any enemy piece from the board, which is then followed by the adversary to also remove one of the player's pieces
    • If after this move, the player's position is still stalemated, the game is a draw
    • Each player can only invoke blockade once
    • Another stalemated position, ends the game
  • Any position that does not allow more pieces to interact also ends the game
  • Wins the player with more captured pieces
