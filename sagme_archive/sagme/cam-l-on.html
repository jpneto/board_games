Caméléon

Date: 2028-07-28 11:53 | Tags: new games

Caméléon, aka Karé Dément, is a 1980s game by Henri Chanteux, published at Asmodee.

The game is played on a cross-shaped board, with a central area of 25 squares and four laterals with ten squares each, and is to be played between two to four players,


the initial setup for four players
notice the hole at the center

The game comes with 25 white tiles, and ten tiles per each one of the four colors.

To prepare the game for two players only, the barriers (the thin five-length pieces seen on the picture) must be placed adjacent to the central area, so that the remaining pieces cannot move inside those lateral areas.

Definition:

  • A move is pushing a line of one or more tiles in the direction of the board hole, a move can only be applied touching a single friendly tile or a white tile

the yellow player pushes a single friendly piece

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player makes a maximum of five moves
    • within a turn, each move must use a different direction from the previous one
  • It is illegal to stalemate the adversary
  • When the adversary has all his pieces, except one, in the opposite lateral area, it is illegal to push back that last piece
  • Wins the player that moves all his pawns into the opposite lateral area
