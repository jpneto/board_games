Road Block

Date: 2029-10-05 08:40 | Tags: older games, tile games

Road Block is a 1945 game by Robert Clark Hollingsworth, published at Leister Game Company.

The game comes with a set of tiles (see below) and the goal is to build a path of at least 12 tiles across.

The ruleset states that players draw new tiles to play, but that could be adapted by having each player start with half of the tiles, to make in a perfect information game.
