Energizer

Date: 2027-01-15 18:00 | Tags: old games, moku

Energizer is a 1984 game by Jim Winslow, published at Spear's Games.

The game is played on the following board:

Each player has three small pieces to play at the intersections, and three large pieces to place inside the squares. They are initially placed in the marked spots on the board.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player moves one friendly piece (of either type) orthogonally or diagonally
    • A piece's moving range is given by the number of adjacent friendly pieces of the other type
      • This implies an isolated piece cannot move
    • A large piece cannot move diagonally if it has to move over a small piece at the intersection
  • Wins the player that makes two 3 in-a-rows (in any one of the eight directions), one with his large pieces, and another with his small pieces

The goal requires a pattern that does not seem easy to achieve, even without the interference of the adversary.
