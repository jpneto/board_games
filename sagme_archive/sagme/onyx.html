Onyx

Date: 2029-06-15 10:27 | Tags: new games, connection

Onyx is a 2000 game by Larry Back, published in Abstract Games Magazine.

This is a connection game played on the following board:

initial setup
North/South is Black, East/West is White

Rules:

  • Initially, one player drops a black piece on an empty intersection, and the other player chooses his color. White moves next.
  • On his turn, a player drops a friendly piece on an empty intersection
    • a piece cannot be placed on the middle of a square if a corner of that square is occupied
    • if the middle square is empty and a piece is placed on the final corner of that square, where both players have two pieces occupying diagonally opposite corners, then the two enemy pieces are captured
  • Wins the player that connects his two opposite sides
    • the four corners of the board belong to both players

It is not common to find a connection game with a capture rule.

The article can be read at Abstract Games Magazine #4 for more details (also at wikipedia). There are several boards, with different sizes, available at BGG.
