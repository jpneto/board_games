Nelaxtam

Date: 2029-09-21 07:00 | Tags: new games

Nelaxtam is a 2005 game by Jorge Gomez Arrausi.

The original game is played on a 12x12 checkered board, including simultaneous movement, a ludeme outside the scope of the blog. 

However, the game has a variant with alternate moves in a 10x10 board, where each player has five pieces, and 50 checkers. For the setup, the white checkers are placed on all white squares, and the black checkers on the black squares

Rules:

  • Player drop their pieces, in turn, on top of checkers of their color
  • On his turn, the player either:
    • move a friendly piece to a diagonally adjacent square containing a checker
    • remove an enemy checker orthogonally adjacent to a friendly piece, which may also include an enemy piece
  • When one player does not have valid moves, the game ends, winning the player with more checkers still on the board.
