Stack

Date: 2030-04-05 14:42 | Tags: old games, stack pieces

Stack is a 1986 game by Steven George Ball, published at BB Games.

The game is played on a 5x8 board,

initial setup

Each player has fifteen stackable pieces. 

Here we consider even a single piece as a stack. The owner of the stack is the player that owns its top piece.

Rules

  • On his turn, the player moves one of his stacks, or part of it (ie, a sub-stack made of the bottom pieces may stay in the same square)
    • The selected stack of N pieces must move orthogonally N squares in a straight line
    • If the moving stack moves to an occupied square, it will stay on top of that stack, and the current player will gain ownership
  • Wins the player that, just before doing his turn, already has a friendly stack on his last row
