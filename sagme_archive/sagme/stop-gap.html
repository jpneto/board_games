Stop Gap

Date: 2027-01-05 20:40 | Tags: old games, checkers

Stop Gap, aka, Strategic, is a 1976 uncredited game, published by Hasbro and Ceji.

The game is played on a 7x7 initially empty, 

 
board after placement phase

Each player has 24 pieces (called guards).

Rules

  • Initially players alternatively place their stones on the board, except the central position (that should start empty)
  • On their first turn, both players must create an Officer (an Officer is two stacked Guards, so the player needs to move one Guard on top of an adjacent friendly Guard)
  • Afterwards, on her turn, the player either moves:
    • A Guard to an adjacent orthogonal empty position, or on top of another friendly Guard making a new Officer
    • An Officer, that slides over a line of empty positions, or jumps over an adjacent enemy Officer (which is captured) landing on the immediate next position that must be empty
      • jumps are multiple and can change direction during jumps
  • A player that stalemates the adversary wins the game (either by blocking or capturing all her pieces)

The rules do not state that jumps are mandatory. This is usually a sign of bad design. Mandatory captures allow for tactical depth, since players can force their adversaries into better positions and capturing sequences. On the other hand, the idea of using Guards as both blocking pieces, and a reserve to build new Officers, is worthwhile and can lead to interesting blocking tactics.
