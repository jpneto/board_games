Novi

Date: 2030-02-22 21:53 | Tags: old games, connection, tile games

Novi is a 1988 uncredited game, published at  The R/L Group.

The game is played on the following 16x16 board:

the red squares are to deal with handicap rules

There are 256 tiles of size 3x3 unit squares, where all different possible tiles are represented:


The box comes with several games, but we are here concerned with the suggested two-player game.

Also, the original rules state that tiles are randomly drawn from a shared reserve. This rules out perfect information. However, it is possible to adapt the rules where each player selects, in turns, a set on N pieces (say, N=8) that needs to be played, until a new batch of N tiles are selected. This allows perfect information and still constrains the player's option.

Rules

  • On his turn, the player drops one tile on an empty 3x3 area, provided that its unit squares match (in terms of colors) with the unit squares from adjacent tiles
    • if the player does not have a valid drop, he must pass his turn
  • Wins the player that makes a continuous orthogonal path of units squares of his color, between two opposite sides (both players can win by connecting South/North or East/West)

It is not certain a path is achievable. Both players can reach a mutual block. The ruleset proposes some ways to solve this problem, neither entirely convincing.
