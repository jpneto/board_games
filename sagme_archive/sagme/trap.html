Trap

Date: 2027-06-25 08:22 | Tags: old games, wall pieces

Trap is a 1972 game by Anita and Eddie Goldfarb, published at Ideal.

The game is played on the following hexagonal board:

Each player has three pieces, and a number of walls that are placed at the edges between hexes. The board edges already have walls.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player either:
    • drops two walls
    • drops one wall and moves a friendly piece
    • moves two pieces
    • moves one piece twice
  • A piece moves like a Chess knight, but cannot jump over walls
  • Wins the player that prevents the adversary of moving his pieces
