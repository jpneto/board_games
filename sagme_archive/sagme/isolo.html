Isolo

Date: 2029-06-08 10:04 | Tags: flip pieces, new games

Isolo is a 1992 uncredited game, published at Blue Zebra.

The board is a 3x11x3 hexagonal board, where each hex has a bicolored balls attached, that player can roll over.


there are also 16 black/yellow balls at each player's side,
to score their points

Rules:

  • Initially, all balls are turned to white, except the central one that is rolled to black
  • On her turn, the player turns one black ball to white, and two adjacent white balls to black
    • each time a white ball is surrounded by black balls, the player scores one point
  • When no more moves are available, wins the player with highest score
