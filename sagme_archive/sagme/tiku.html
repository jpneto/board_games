Tiku

Date: 2028-03-31 12:50 | Tags: new games

Tiku, aka Clinch, is a 1993 game by Reiner Knizia, published at Spielbox magazine

The game is played on a 6x6 board,


initial setup

Each player has 14 regular pieces, and six large pieces.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player orthogonally slides a friendly large piece over a sequence of squares free from large pieces (large pieces can slide over regular pieces)
    • After moving the large piece, all squares it passes over (including its initial square) are covered with friendly regular stones
    • If there are previous regular pieces on those squares (of either color), each is returned to his respective player; this includes the final square where the large piece stops
  • Wins the player that has his 14 regular pieces on board 

A strategy tip from BGG: While it is important to place as many stones on your turn as possible, sometimes it is more important to make moves which force your opponent to take back some of their already placed markers. 

The ruleset suggested a variant that each large piece could perform on special diagonal move, once per match. That idea was largely expanded in a 2009's Tiku reimplementation, Battle of Olympus, with several piece's powers representing the gods of the Greek pantheon.
