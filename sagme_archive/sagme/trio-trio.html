Trio Trio

Date: 2029-11-16 15:07 | Tags: new games, moku

Trio Trio is a 2004 game by Frank Stark, published at Gerhards Spiel und Design.

The game is played on a 5x5 board (the inner field), with four extra segments 1x5 near the edges (the outer pits):

Each player has twelve pieces.

Rules

  • Initially, players alternate dropping six of their stones on the outer pits
  • Each pit can contain a maximum of three pieces per pit
  • On his turn a player can:
    • jump a piece from the outer pit over a straight line of pieces to the next free inner square
      in the same row. The pieces jump over a continuous sequence of pieces of either color. 
    • pull a stone already in the inner field, horizontally or vertically, to an adjacent free square
    • drop a new piece from the player's reserve in an empty square on the outer pit
    • moves a friendly piece from an outer pit to another empty square in an outer pit
  • Wins the player that makes two 3 in-a-row inside the inner pit (the two patterns cannot share pieces)
