Trinidad

Date: 2029-02-23 21:29 | Tags: checkers, new games, moku

Trinidad is a 2003 game by Maureen Hiron, published by Pin International.

The game is played on a 7x7 board, with a central 3x3 area,


the initial setup

Each player has ten pieces.

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player picks a friendly piece and either:
    • moves it to an orthogonal adjacent empty square
    • uses it to jump over an diagonally adjacent enemy piece, landing on the immediate next square that must be empty
      • jumps can be multiple, and are not mandatory
  • Wins the player that makes a 3 in-a-row in the central area

This is a mix of checkers and moku games. An interesting design, given the simplicity of its rules.
