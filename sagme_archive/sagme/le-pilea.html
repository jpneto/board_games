Le Pilea

Date: 2027-03-05 13:43 | Tags: stack pieces, new games

Le Pilea is a undated (c.1995-2005) game by Patrik Carpentier.

The game is played on a 5x5, with this initial setup:

Each player has five stackable pieces.

The stack ownership is determined by the color of its top piece.

Rules:

  • Initially, player alternate placing their pieces on empty squares
  • On her turn, a player move a friendly piece/stack horizontally or vertically to an adjacent square
    • the entire stack can be moved to empty square
    • the top piece at the stack can be moved to empty square
    • the top piece can be moved on top of an adjacent stack (regardless of ownership), but only if their height different is zero or one
      • exception: the central stack can only be accessed via stacks at least as high
    • Capture by custodian: if one enemy stack is trapped between two of the player's stacks, the player removes its top piece and places on any empty square (so, the board always have the ten pieces of both players)
      • exception: the capture rule does not apply for the central stack
  • Wins the player that places a stone at the central stack
