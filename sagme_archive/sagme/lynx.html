Lynx

Date: 2029-07-27 07:12 | Tags: new games, connection, pencil & paper

Lynx is a 2004 game by Dan Troyka and Cameron Browne, published in Browne's 2005 book Connection Games.

The game is pencil and paper game based on Dots'n'Boxes, and played in a nxn grid, typically 8x8.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player connects two dots such that:
    • The dots were not previously connected with each other
    • The connection does not go thru an enclosed/shaded area
    • Both dots cannot be part of four connections
  • When an area is enclosed, the player shades it with his color
  • One player owns the North-South edges, the other player owns the East-West edges
  • A player wins if there is a shaded path of his color between his two opposite edges

The rule forbidding the four connections on a single dot implies, quite elegantly, that is always possible to find a winner, since it is not possible to create a blocking position!

Notice that, contrary to Dots'n'Boxes, a player that closes an area does not move again. This makes the game more interesting, allowing for tactical traps, and improving the game's drama.
