Tower of Power

Date: 2028-05-12 12:24 | Tags: old games, stack pieces

Tower of Power is a 1976 game by Michael Baldwin, published at Games & Puzzles.

The game is played in a 8x8 board with this Halma-like setup,

Each player has 15 pieces.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player picks an orthogonal or diagonal line of two or more friendly pieces, either single pieces or pieces on top of stacks, and piles them at one of its extremes
    • the line then is laid down in a different orthogonal or diagonal direction, one piece per square (the bottom piece stays in the same place)
      • the player must choose a direction that keeps all pieces on board
    • any piece, being laid down, that lands in an occupied square by:
      • an enemy piece, the enemy piece is captured; if the piece belongs to stack, the remaining stack is unaffected (basically, the top piece just changed color)
      • a friendly piece, the new piece is stacked on top of it
  • Wins the player that stalemates the adversary

Note that a single piece cannot move just by itself. So, one way to win is by making the remaining adversary pieces isolated from each other.
