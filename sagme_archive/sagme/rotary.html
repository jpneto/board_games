Rotary

Date: 2027-12-31 09:08 | Tags: rotating pieces, new games

Rotary, or Rotaryh, is a c.2000 game by Nagy László, published online.

The game is played in the following board and setup:

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player selects a hex with six neighbors (so, not a hex at the board's edge) and rotate the neighbors any multiple of 60º
    • however, the hex rotation is only valid if the player has more pieces than the adversary on those six neighbors
    • the hex itself, the pivot, can be empty or occupied by pieces of either player
  • Wins the player that occupies the center triangle with three friendly pieces

Blue can win the game by rotating her pieces 60º left,
around the white marked hex
