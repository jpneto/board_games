Towers of Hamburg

Date: 2028-04-21 07:11 | Tags: new games, stack pieces

Towers of Hamburg is a 1998 game by Nkem Benjamin Soberekon, published at Beson.

The game is played on a 6x6 board,

Each piece has 18 stackable pieces, which are initially placed on the board in a diagonal pattern

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player either moves:
    • an entire stack, with two or more pieces, to an empty square
    • a friendly piece (which might be on top of a stack) onto a friendly piece that is on its own level
      • this means that to make a stack of size N, the player needs at least two stacks of size N-1
  • If the adversary has the tallest stack, all squares in the same row and column cannot move (there is a blockade)
    • pieces and stacks outside can pile up/move into a blockade
    • the only way to lift a blockade is to match that tallest stack
  • Wins the player that makes a stack of height six, or stalemates the adversary
