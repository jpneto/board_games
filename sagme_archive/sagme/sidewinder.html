Sidewinder

Date: 2029-01-26 14:46 | Tags: new games

Sidewinder is a 2001 game by Dan Troyka.

The game is played in the following board and setup:

Each player has nine pieces.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player slides a friendly piece along the forward diagonals
    • Captures are by replacement
  • Wins the player that moves a friendly piece to the opposite corner

In this next position, Black just slided a piece to a2 and wins in four moves. White answers with e1:a2, which Black replies with g4-b2. There's nothing White can do to prevent b2-e1 and, finally, e1-d1.

As stated in its Zillions page:

The principal game strategy consists of getting under your opponent's defenses. This happens when you move a snake onto a diagonal that is behind most of your opponent's snakes. The opponent can defend only by moving snakes out from the snakepit area (the home corner), which substantially weakens the opponent's defense. The converse of this strategy is that you should try to keep a snake in your snakepit, with other snakes close by, as long as possible.
