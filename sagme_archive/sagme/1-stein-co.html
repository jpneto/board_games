1 Stein + Co.

Date: 2028-05-05 11:47 | Tags: new games, tile games

1 Stein + Co., aka Le Bloq, is a 2003 game by Niek Neuwahl, published at Holzinsel and Goliath Games.

The game is played in the following board, where players drop 2x3x4 cuboids,

There are 40 pieces to be used by both players. The board starts initially empty.

Rules: 

  • On her turn, the player drops a piece on empty squares of the board
    • The first piece cannot touch the board edge  
    • Each remaining piece must touch some area of another piece on board
    • The piece can be dropped in any of the three axis (using 2x3, 2x4 or 3x4 areas)
    • The piece cannot touch another piece with the same height, unless the move scores points (see below)
  • If the dropped piece produces one or more enclosed areas (surrounded by pieces and/or board edges) in shapes that cannot be used to place other pieces, the player scores 1 point per enclosed square
  • Wins the player with highest score

The rule of relaxing height differences when scoring will produce matches with low scores. Perhaps it is best to play with this restriction, to increase the score count and, thus, the game's drama.

Another game using tiles to surround and score empty areas is 2005's Zoned by Niek Neuwahl, and published at Family Games, Inc.

The BGG description:

A wooden game with a very limited print run. The pieces in the game are a single square attached to a triangle with a base twice the size of the square side (like a house with a full roof but only one half below). Similar to 1 Stein + Co, pieces are placed on a grid with the goal of blocking off territory. Cubes are placed in the closed in spaces and the player who can place most of their cubes wins.
