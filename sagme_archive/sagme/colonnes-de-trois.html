Colonnes de Trois

Date: 2028-04-14 19:51 | Tags: old games, stack pieces, moku

Colonnes de Trois is a 1986 game, published at Jeux et Stratègie #39.

The game is played on a 3x3 board. Each player has eight stackable pieces. 

Definitions:

  • A free piece is any piece not under other pieces. 

Rules:

  • Initially, each player drops a friendly piece on an empty square, or on top of another piece (of either color) up to a maximum height of three pieces
  • After all pieces are on the board, each player moves a friendly free piece to any orthogonal adjacent square (again, restricted to a maximum height of three)
    • if player cannot move, he passes his turn
  • ko rule: the player can not regularly return to the same positions [the exact procedure is not explained, but let's assume a Go-like Ko rule]
  • Wins the player that achieves a stack with three friendly stones

This is an interesting take on moku games, played in the area of Tic-Tac-Toe. Trapping pieces to move again, and force zugzwangs make it a very nice little board game.
