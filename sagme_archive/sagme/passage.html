Passage

Date: 2028-10-13 12:52 | Tags: old games, tile games

Passage is a 1976 game by Mark Berger, published at Games & Puzzles.

The game is played on a 20x20, initially empty.

Each player has two sets of the following polyominoes (each player has them in a different color),

Definition:

  • Protection - a piece as a level of protection equal to the number of shared points with adjacent friendly pieces 

piece A has a level of protection of 4,
while piece B as a level of protection of 3

Rules:

  • On the first turn, the first player places a friendly domino outside the 10x10 center area
  • Afterwards, on his turn, the player drops two friendly pieces on empty squares
  • Capture: if a piece can be placed in a position covering at least two squares of an enemy piece, in such a way that its level of protection would be higher than the enemy's, then the enemy piece is captured and the piece is placed in that position
  • Wins the player that makes an orthogonal path of friendly pieces between his two opposite edges (one player is North/South, the other is East/West)
    • however, the adversary has one last chance to capture one of the pieces making the path. If he is able to, the game continues

The game also includes a scoring system (see the next review for details).
