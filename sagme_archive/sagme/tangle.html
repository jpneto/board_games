Tangle

Date: 2028-06-30 20:27 | Tags: old games, tile games

Tangle is a 1964 uncredited game, published at Selchow & Righter.

Each player as a set of 29 pieces that fit into one or more hex (or hexes) edges. These are the shapes available and how much each is worth:

i.e., each piece is worth the number of edges it covers

Rules:

  • On the player’s first turn, they must drop a friendly tile occupying at least one edge of the center hex (marked with a star).
  • On his turn, the player drops one friendly piece on a sequence of empty edges
    • the new piece must connect to another piece on board (of either color)
  • If a player surrounds a hex with pieces of his color, he scores and then plays again:
    • The score for that hex is is the worth of the current played piece, plus extra points for the type of the surrounded hex (cf. next diagram)
  • Wins the player with the highest scoring 
    • also, if a player achieves 50 points, he wins immediately
