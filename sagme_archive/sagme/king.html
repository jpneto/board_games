King

Date: 2030-08-30 22:04 | Tags: new games

King is a 1994 game by Reiner Knizia, published at W. Nostheide Verlag.


game setup

Each player has eleven pieces, four kings and seven soldiers.

Rules

  • On her turn, the player moves one friendly piece to one of the three forward hexes
    • Soldiers can capture enemy kings or soldiers by replacement
    • Kings can capture enemy kings by replacement
    • If a soldier reaches the last row, it is removed from play
  • Wins the player that moves a friendly king to the last row, or captures all enemy kings

The game was originally published in the Spielbox magazine, issue 1994 #3.
