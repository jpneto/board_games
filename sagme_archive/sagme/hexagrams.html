Hexagrams

Date: 2026-11-13 17:57 | Tags: old games

Hexagrams is a 1988 game by Marv Nelson which self-published it.

The game is played on a tiling of hexagons and triangles. Each player has 16 tall pieces (the Triads) and five flat disks (the Hexads) starting in the following setup:

Rules:

  • A player must do one of the following actions, in decreasing priority:
    • using a hexad to capture an adjacent enemy hexad, this is possible only if the two triangles between them are empty; capturing hexads is optional
    • using a triad to capture an adjacent enemy triad; capturing triads is mandatory
    • move one friendly piece, hexad or triad, to an adjacent empty cell (hexads can only move when the two adjacent triangles are empty)
      • triads can only move forward
      • a triad can be moved out of the board, at the player's last row
  • Wins the player that either captures all enemy hexads, or by stalemating the adversary
