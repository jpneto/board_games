Linkage

Date: 2029-07-06 07:41 | Tags: dominoes, new games

Linkage is a 2002 game by Ethan Larson, published at nestorgames.

The game is played on a 7x7 board, initially empty.

There are 24 shared dominoes, six for each one of four colors. There's also a black marker.

One player is called More, the other is called Less, denoting their different winning goals. 

Definition:

  • A group is a set of connected dominoes of the same color

Rules:

  • Initially, the black marker is placed at the center, More starts playing.
  • Players take turns dropping one domino into two empty squares
    • the current domino must not be edge adjacent to the (eventual) domino placed in the previous turn by the opponent
    • if there are not valid move, the player passes
  • When both player pass, the game ends
    • More wins if there are twelve or more groups, otherwise Lose wins

There is a later mention of a pie rule:

At the time this is being printed, Linkage strategy has developed somewhat and shows a slight advantage for the More player. So the following rule can be played both to mitigate this advantage, and to add some variety to the starting configuration. 

When beginning the game, one player starts by placing the black counter anywhere on the board. Then the other player chooses whether to play More or Fewer. Play then continues normally, with whoever is More going first as usual.

However, I'm not sure if this makes any difference. Perhaps one player placing three dominoes, and the other deciding who starts, would be better.
