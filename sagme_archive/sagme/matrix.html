Matrix

Date: 2026-12-18 22:29 | Tags: old games, moku

Matrix is a 1974 uncredited game, published at Knots.

The game is played on a 4x4 board:

There are four pieces for each one of the four different colors.

Rules:

  • One player has three pieces of two color, the other has three pieces from the other two colors
  • On her turn, the player drops a friendly piece on an empty square
    • however, no two pieces of the same color can be adjacent (orthogonally or diagonally)
    • if the player does not have a legal move available, she must pass
  • Wins the player that makes a 4 in-a-row with four different colors; 
    • if both players pass, the game is a draw

The official rules propose a second game with more pieces and less drop restrictions:

A moku game with added color for originality, but the fact that both players need the opposite pieces to win, the game seems to fall into cold positions, where the players either destroy promising sequences with repeated colors, or are forced to allow the adversary to win (that is, if the position does not end on a quick and frustrating draw).
