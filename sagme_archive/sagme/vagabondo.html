Vagabondo

Date: 2028-07-07 21:08 | Tags: old games, tile games

Vagabondo is a 1979 game by Peter Forbes and Brian Taylor, published at Invicta.

The game is played on a 14x14 board, and the box includes 36 tiles:

Each player takes an equal set of 18 pieces.

The squares marked with crosses are doubling squares

Rules:

  • In the first turn, players place a friendly piece on empty squares, but not on the doubling squares
  • Afterwards, the player on his turn, drops a friendly piece on empty squares
    • pieces cannot touch other pieces of the same color shade
    • a piece dropped on top of a doubling square must be adjacent to at least one piece
  • Each piece dropped scores points:
    • a piece is worth its area in units squares
    • if the piece is over a doubling square, the score is the sum of the piece plus all the pieces it touches, multiplied by two!
  • Wins the player with the highest score

Interestingly, the rules suggest the use of the Doubling Dice of Backgammon, a rare but quite nice suggestion.
