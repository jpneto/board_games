Triad

Date: 2026-12-25 07:28 | Tags: old games

Triad is a 1986 game by Don Cornelson, published by Game Concepts.

The game is played in the following board:

Players have the main pieces (the jewels) that start at the three circles on the left/right edge of the board, and move into the board triangles. They also have ten pieces (the gems) that are placed at the intersections. The rules are extensive and be be read here.

The game seems rich in tactical variety, since there are three ways of winning which needs gems and jewels, with their different powers, to interact. Each win condition is different, players can win by moving a jewel to the other side of the board, or by custodian capturing gems, or even by using gems to completely block jewels. There are quite a number of rules but they are not too complex. Another underrated abstract game.
