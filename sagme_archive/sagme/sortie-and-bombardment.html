Sortie and Bombardment

Date: 2029-04-27 10:33 | Tags: wall pieces, new games

Sortie is a 2005 game by Greg Schmidt.

The game is played on a 8x7 board with the following setup:


Each player has 14 pieces (the planes).

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player moves one of his pieces
    • planes move one step vertically or diagonally forward
    • if a plane moves to a square occupied by an enemy plane, both explode and that square becomes unpassable
    • a plane cannot move over a friendly plane
  • Wins the player that first moves one plane to his opposite row, or stalemates the adversary

In the next diagram, South already has a winning advantage. By exploding the plane at g4, South still has five moves left with f1 and g1, while North only has three moves (and he moves next).

This reminds me of 2001's Breakthrough with exploding pieces.

To my knowledge, the concept of kamizake pieces creating walls is quite original. 

There's a ZRF to play Sortie.

Another Breakthrough-like game is 2003's Bombardment by Chris Huntoon,

Here the players control missiles, that can also move forward, or just explode. When a missile explodes, all orthogonally and diagonally adjacent pieces (of either color) are captured. Wins the player that moves a missile to the last row, or destroy all enemy missiles.

There's a ZRF to play Bombardment.
