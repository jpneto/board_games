Rules
  • During the game, players alternate turns. A piece may either:
    • move one space along the drawn lines.
    • jump over an adjacent friendly piece, landing on the immediate next cell that must be empty; multiple jumps are allowed.
    • jump over an adjacent enemy piece, landing on the immediate next cell that must be empty; after jumping, one of the opponent’s pieces is placed on the jumper’s old position.
      • if there are several possible jumps, the player must continue jumping, but may choose the most advantageous route.
  • The game ends when one player has placed all their pieces on the board, or when the board is completely filled with pieces. 
    • The winner is the player who has managed to place more pieces.
