Kengi

Date: 2029-07-13 11:59 | Tags: old games, stack pieces

Kengi is a 1988 game by Claude Leroy, published at Fi Du Dé.

The game is played on a 5x5 board, with the following setup,

The game has 25 shared pieces, five pieces per each one of five colors.

Definitions:

  • A piece is playable only if it is the closest to the player in each column (so initially, one player can only play pieces a1 to e1, and the other player can only play pieces a5 to e5)

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player picks a playable piece and places it on top of a piece or stack
    • a stack cannot have two or more pieces with the same color
    • if the playable piece is in a stack, the player can also move the entire stack
    • a piece can be placed in an empty square only if it was taken from the top of a stack 
    • a player cannot move the piece/stack moved by the opponent in the previous turn
  • Wins the player that makes a stack with all five colors
