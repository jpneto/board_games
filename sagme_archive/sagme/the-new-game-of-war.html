The New Game of War

Date: 2030-10-11 13:16 | Tags: older games, checkers

The New Game of War is a 1892 uncredited game, published by Feltham & Co.

Each player has nine pieces, placed on the player's first row. The center board is called the citadel.

Rules

  • On his turn, the player moves a friendly piece
    • a piece moves to any orthogonal empty circle
    • pieces can capture adjacent enemy pieces by jumping over and landing on the immediate next circle that must be empty; captures are mandatory and multiple
  • Captured pieces are swapped (when both players have them), and placed on any of the player's colored circles
  • A piece when entering the citadel is removed from play, unless the piece must complete a capturing sequence (and, so, jumps out of the citadel)
  • The player that removes all his pieces, wins the game
