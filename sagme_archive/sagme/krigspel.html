Krigspel

Date: 2029-03-23 14:26 | Tags: chess, new games

Krigspel is a 2005 game by Markus Salo, published online.

The game is played on a 7x7 board, initially empty.

Each player has five soldiers (the infantry), two knights (the cavalry) and a royal piece (the head quarter).

Rules:

  • Initially, players take turns dropping their pieces on empty squares. First their soldiers, then their knights and finally their head quarters
  • Afterwards, each player moves a friendly piece
    • soldiers move like Chess kings
    • cavalry move like Chess knights
    • head quarters move one step orthogonally (they cannot capture)
  • Captures are by replacement
  • Wins the player that captures the enemy head quarter

In the diagram, Black just moved his soldier c5-c4 winning the game. Despite having two knights at c2 and e1, White is unable to fight the black threat. Playing a3-a2 is answered with c4-b3+. If e1-d2, then c4-c3 forking both knights.

Soldiers are very powerful in this interestingly simple chess variant.
