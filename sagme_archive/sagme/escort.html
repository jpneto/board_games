Escort

Date: 2029-08-17 12:42 | Tags: old games

Escort: The Game of Guys and Gals is a 1955 uncredited game, published by Parker Brothers.


There are nine supporting pieces (guys) and three main pieces (gals).

Rules

  • On her turn, the player moves one friendly piece
    • All pieces can move an adjacent empty square
    • Supporting pieces can capture adjacent enemy supporting pieces, by jumping over them and landing in the immediate next cell (that must be empty)
  • Wins the player that first moves her three main pieces to their three homes at the last row (one main piece per home)
