Xexex

Date: 2027-09-24 07:30 | Tags: chess, old games, tile games

Xexex is a 1989 game by Patrick Carpentier, published online.

The game is played on a 8x8 board,

Each player has the same set of 16 tiles,

The 'point' tile is a royal piece.

Rules:

  • Initially players place their 16 pieces as they wish on their first two rows
  • Each player, on their turn, either:  
    • rotates any friendly piece by a 90º
    • moves any friendly piece one square in one of the directions indicated by the tile
      • e.g., the T piece (upwards) can move down, right or left, but cannot move up
      • e.g., the point tile cannot move nor rotate
      • pieces capture by replacement
  • Wins the player that captures the point tile
