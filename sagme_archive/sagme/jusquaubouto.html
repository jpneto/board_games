Jusquaubouto

Date: 2030-04-19 08:32 | Tags: checkers, old games

Jusquaubouto is a pre-1940s game by Edmond Bertrand.

This is a Checkers variant played on a 10x10 board, with 15 pieces per players.

The rules of Checkers apply except on the following:

  • Each player alternately moves a piece along a diagonal as far as it can go (jusqu’au bout), i.e., until it hits the edge of the board or another piece (friend or enemy)
  • At the beginning of the game, White must start by playing a piece on the edge (there are four possible opening moves)
  • Wins the player that cannot move (so, the last player that moved loses the game)
