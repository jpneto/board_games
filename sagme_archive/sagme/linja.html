Linja

Date: 2029-06-01 08:36 | Tags: boardless, new games, traditional games

Linja is a 2003 game by Steffen Mühlhäuser, published at Steffen Spiele.

The game's playing area is defined by a set of sticks,


This is a race/scoring game, where the amount of pieces in a row determines the moving range of those pieces.
