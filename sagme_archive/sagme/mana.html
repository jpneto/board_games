Mana

Date: 2029-03-02 10:23 | Tags: chess, old games

Mana is a 1988 game by Claude Leroy, published at Fi Du Dé.

The game is played on a 6x6 board, where each square markings have an effect on piece movement,

Each player has five regular pieces (the ronin) and one royal piece (the damyo).
