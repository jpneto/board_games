Rescue

Date: 2026-09-18 13:11 | Tags: old games, stack pieces

Rescue is a 1983 game by R. Wayne Schmittberger, published in GAMES #36.

This is a stack-based game, played on a 9x5 board, with nine pieces each. Players need to reach their home squares to rescue their pieces:


Schmittberger was the author of the 1992 classic book New Rules for Classic Games.
