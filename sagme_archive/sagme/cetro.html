Cetro

Date: 2030-07-05 06:52 | Tags: older games

Cetro is a 1903 uncredited game, published at J. W. Spear & Son.

This is a Halma-like game, played on the following 9x9 board:


the game' setup

Each player has a total of 14 pieces of three different colors (five red, four green, five blue).

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player moves a friendly piece.
    • A piece can jump diagonally forwards over another piece, landing on the immediate next square that must be empty; jumps are not multiple
    • A piece can also move diagonally backwards to an empty square
  • The adversary may request the player must make a jump, if any is available (if several are available, the player is free to choose)
  • If a player stalemates the adversary, he continues moving until the adversary can move again
  • Wins the player that positions her pieces in the last three rows, just like in the setup: each piece on a same color square
    • If a stalemate position occurs where neither player can move, wins the player who made the last move
