Choko

Date: 2029-02-16 20:47 | Tags: traditional games

Choko is a traditional game from Gambia. 

The game is played on a 5x5 board, initially empty.

Each player has twelve pieces.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player either:
    • drops a friendly piece on an empty square
      • if a player drops a piece, then the adversary is forced to also drop a piece (that second drop, however, does not force the first player to continue dropping)
    • moves a friendly stone to an orthogonal adjacent empty square 
    • uses a friendly stone to jump over an adjacent enemy piece, landing on the immediate next square that must be empty
      •  the jumped piece is captured, and then the moving player chooses a second enemy piece to also capture
  • If a player gets stalemated, the adversary captures all remaining pieces on the board [not sure if this is an original rule]
  • Wins the player that captures all enemy stones

Notice that the rules give the initiative to the first player that starts a dropping sequence. So, while there are still pieces to enter, a decision to move/capture on the board has the cost of giving the initiative to the adversary. An elegant mechanism to add tension and drama into the game dynamics.
