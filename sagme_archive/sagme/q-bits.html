Q-bits

Date: 2028-07-21 07:59 | Tags: old games, tile games

Q-bits is a 1976 game by Ivan Moscovich, published at Orda Industries Ltd.

The game is played on a 8x8 board. There are 16 tiles of area 2x2 made of four unit square using two colors. The 16 tiles are: two solid color (one white, one black), two checkered, four half color, eight quarter color. Their values are:

Rules:

  • One player makes two sets of eight tiles each, the other player chooses which set to play and starts second
  • The first tile is placed anywhere on the board
  • On his turn, the player drops a tile that must be adjacent to at least one tile already on board, and the colors of all the edges must match
  • For each dropped tile, the player scores its value (see diagram above)
    • notice that players must define one absolute direction, 'the board's North', in order to evaluate the score of each tile (since the score depends on the tile's orientation)
  • At the end of the game, each non-placed tile scores the negative of its maximum possible value
  • Wins the player with the highest score
