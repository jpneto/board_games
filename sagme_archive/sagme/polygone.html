Polygone

Date: 2028-02-25 11:36 | Tags: rotating pieces, new games

Polygone is a 2004 game by Fabien Vandenbussche, published at Facabo.

The game is played on the edges of a 4x6x4 hexagonal board

 
initial setup

Each player has twelve pieces with a shape that fit at the edges.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player moves one friendly piece.
    • A piece moves by rotating over one of its two vertices (it can rotate outside the board)
    • When rotating, the piece cannot cross other pieces
      • however, it can rotate from one side of a triangle to the other, if the third edge is occupied
    • Pieces cannot move backwards, i.e., the moving end must approach the enemy corner or, at least, keep its current distance
  • Capture: if, after the rotation, a close polygon appears then:
    • Nothing happens if
      • all edges are of the player's color
      • both players have the same number of pieces at the edges
      • there are other pieces in the interior of the polygon
    • otherwise, the pieces of the player in minority are removed from the board
    • If there are several polygons, the current player chooses the order to deal with them
    • If new polygons appear meanwhile, those must also be dealt with
  • Wins the player that either captures seven enemy pieces, or moves one piece to the opposite corner
