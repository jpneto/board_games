Mako Carrés

Date: 2027-02-05 08:46 |

Mako Carrés is a board game published by Mako, which I don't know the date (it is pre-2003) nor the author.

There are 36 pieces per player (the game can be played up to four players).

Rules: 

  • On her turn, the player drops a friendly piece on an empty position
  • When a player forms a square, they remove the opponent's pieces that are inside it
    • The squares can be of any size and orientation (considering the green or red lines)
  • When all pieces are dropped, wins the player with more pieces on board (or, equivalently, with more captures)

Even against a single adversary, it's not hard to eliminate a threat, since it takes just one piece to counter a three-piece effort of building almost a square. It seems more probable that players will fail to notice a threat, given all the intersecting lines that make the board.
