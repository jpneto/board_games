Panzerschlacht

Date: 2030-07-26 07:51 | Tags: older games, checkers

Panzerschlacht (Tank Battle) is a 1935 uncredited game, published at Hugo Gräfe Verlag.

game's setup

Rules:

  • Each tank may only move on fields of the same color, which is also shown by the starting field. 
  • Tanks must always be aligned in the direction they shoot.
    • Light tanks move 1–6 squares
    • Heavy tanks move 1–4 squares
  • Tanks may only shoot in a straight line forward.
    • Light tanks shoot at the third square ahead
    • Heavy tanks shoot at the third or fourth square ahead
    • A shot must be made immediately after the move. The tank stays on the square it moved to and shoots from there.
    • Any enemy tank located on the square being targeted (third or fourth square) is removed from the game.
    • If one side overlooks a shooting opportunity, the other side may — regardless of turn — remove the unshot-at enemy tank from the game.
    • Capturing an enemy tank by moving your own tank into its firing line (without shooting) does not count as a shot.
  • Cover
    • Tanks that are directly behind one another in a straight shooting line and are of the same color count as covered.
    • They can only be hit from behind or the side.
    • Tanks in cover can shoot from behind their front tank.
    • Uncovered tanks can be shot at from their starting position.
  • Dark Green Fields
    • These represent tank trenches or armored positions and must be bypassed.
    • However, you may shoot over these fields — just like you can shoot over friendly tanks standing in a shooting line — but not if the field is a target.
  • Encirclement Maneuver
    • Each tank that penetrates the enemy formation (i.e., reaches the second row of enemy fields), turns around — and if possible — shoots immediately.
    • On the next turn, the tank can return to the battlefield and shoot at the enemy — including from behind.With multiple tanks, it is possible to encircle and trap the enemy using this method.
  • The winner is the player who completely destroys the enemy tank unit.
    • If the forces on both sides are so diminished that neither has a realistic chance of destroying the enemy reserves, the winner is the player who has succeeded in penetrating the enemy formation and getting behind the opponent with more (or stronger) tanks.
