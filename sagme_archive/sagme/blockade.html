Blockade

Date: 2029-08-10 12:19 | Tags: chess, older games

Blockade, aka Naval Chess, is a 1870 game by Nicholas Joseph Vander Weyde.

The game is played on a hexagonal diamond grid (similar to Hex) with size 8.

Each player has one Admiral (a royal piece like the Chess King), three Commodores (like Knights), and four Captains (like Rooks). The initial setup is shown in the previous image.

Rules

  • On his turn the player moves a friendly piece:
    • Admirals move to any adjacent empty hex
    • Commodores move two hexes landing on an empty hex of the same color (they can jump over pieces)
    • Captains slides over a line of empty hexes
  • There are no captures
  • Wins the player that surrounds/immobilizes the opponent's Admiral
