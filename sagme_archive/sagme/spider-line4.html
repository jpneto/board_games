Spider Line4

Date: 2029-03-30 13:45 | Tags: new games, moku

Connect 4 is a classical abstract game, one of the most famous moku games.

Spider Line4 is a 2002 variant by Filip Rachunek, published online.

The idea is that, instead of gravity pulling down pieces, pieces can 'stick' to any border, or to any piece already placed.


the marks show where is it possible to move next

According to brainking.com (where is it possible to play this game), the first player has a win rate of 58.2%, so a quite strong first player advantage. As a comparison, Connect-4 has just a 51.7% advantage for the first player. This advantage is comparable to Gomoku, with 57.3% win rate, which means, this variant should have some balancing mechanism. As a curiosity, Pente has 54.3% win rate, while Connect-6 has barely any advantage at all with a win rate of 50,9%.
