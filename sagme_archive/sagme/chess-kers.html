Chess-kers

Date: 2030-11-15 12:44 | Tags: old games

Chess-kers is a 1969 game by Donald E. Van Elk, published at  The Game Crafter.

 

Each player starts with five pieces, which have two colors, one per side (like Othello pieces). 

The game is a mix of Shogi and Checkers, where pieces are captured when landed by another piece that is jumping. Captured pieces are flipped, and go to the attackers' reserve where they can be reused.
