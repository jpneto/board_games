Hepta

Date: 2028-10-20 13:25 | Tags: old games, tile games

Hepta, aka Cover-Up, is a 1974 game by Alex Randolph, published at Otto Maier Verlag.

The board is 7x7 board with a pre-defined color pattern:

There are also 14 shared trominoes: seven 1x3 and seven L-shaped.

Rules:

  • Players choose one of the seven available colors on the board
  • On her turn, the turn, the player drops a tile over the board without covering the chosen color
  • Wins the player that moves last 

The game also includes seven puzzles, one per color, where the player must place all 14 tiles without covering the respective color.
