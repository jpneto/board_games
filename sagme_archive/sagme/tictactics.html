TicTactics

Date: 2027-02-19 10:29 | Tags: old games, moku

TicTactics is a 1985 game by Mike Ferguson, published at Innovention.

The game is played on nine 3x3 board, with four extra middle squares (the migration squares):

Each player has pieces of two colors (19 pieces per color). One player has blue and green pieces, the other has red and orange.

Rules:

  • Players drop, on their turns, a friendly piece on an empty square.
    • The pieces are placed following this color order cycle: blue, red, green, orange.
  • Wins the player that makes a 3 in-a-row with pieces of the same color
    • Players can use the migration squares to win, but they must be use by the middle piece of the winning row (eg, b2,c3,d4 would not win, while c3,d4,e5 would win)
