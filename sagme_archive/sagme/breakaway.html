Breakaway

Date: 2027-03-12 14:47 | Tags: old games

Breakaway is a 1983 game by Sid Sackson, published at GAMES #41.

The rules state that a player to win cannot have his seven pieces adjacent to each other. While possible, that is not easy to achieve with just 30 intersections having orthogonal and diagonal connections. An easier variant would be to disallow diagonal connections regarding the winning condition (or, in at the middle ground, disallow just one type of diagonal, making the board hexagonal wrt deciding the winner).
