Panic Button

Date: 2026-09-07 15:24 | Tags: old games, checkers

Panic Button is a 1978 uncredited game, published at Mego Co.

The game is played on a 8x8 board which is split in four rotating quadrants:

 

This is a Checkers-like game, with multiple short-jumps to capture adversary pieces. The original element is the possibility for a player to rotate the board within itself.
