Irensei

Date: 2027-01-22 21:05 | Tags: old games, moku

Irensei is a 1987 game by Toki Higashi and Masaki Ida.

The game is played in the middle 15x15 area of a Go board:


Blue moved and won the match

Rules:

  • The Go rules apply, except:
  • Suicide is allowed if it implies a win
  • Wins the player that makes a 7 in-a-row
    • Black (the first player) is not allowed to win with a 8+ in-a-row (aka, overline)

The game can be played at Abstract Play.
