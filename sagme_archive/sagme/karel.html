Karel

Date: 2027-12-10 10:00 | Tags: rotating pieces, boardless, new games, tile games

Karel is a 1996 game by Patrick Carpentier, published online.

The game does not start with a board, the arena is built progressively using tiles like this,

Each player has ten pieces. There are nine tiles in a shared reserve.

Rules:

  • Initially, one tile is placed the playing area
  • On his turn, the player either:
    • drops a friendly piece on an empty circle
    • drops a tile, from the reserve, adjacent to another tile (orientation doesn't matter)
    • moves one friendly piece to an adjacent square, accordingly to the lines
    • rotates a tile 90º, 180º or 270º (regardless of how many pieces it contains)
  • Ko rule: a player cannot change a tile manipulated by the adversary in the previous turn
  • Wins the player that joins his ten pieces in one connected group
    • if both players connect their pieces at the same itme, wins the player that made the last move
