Pilare

Date: 2028-12-15 21:12 | Tags: new games, stack pieces

Pilare is a 2005 game by Jorge Gómez Arrausi, published online.

The game is played on a 7x7 board,


Each player has ten pieces (red for one player, white for the other) and 36 black neutral pieces. The pieces must be stackable.

A stack consists of one or more pieces. The color of top piece defines its ownership.

the initial setup (used blue instead of black)

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player picks one owned stack and places the bottom piece in an orthogonal adjacent square, continuing to do that for each piece on the stack (so the top piece is the last to be placed)
    • for each new piece, the player can turn left or right, but not backwards (it is possible to drop a piece on the original square only if the stack has, at least, height four)
  • Wins the player that stalemates the adversary

The stack redistribution is the same sowing mechanism of Mancala, but I would not consider Pilare a Macala variant due to its stalemating goal.
