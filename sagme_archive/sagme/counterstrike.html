Counterstrike

Date: 2027-04-23 09:39 | Tags: old games

Counterstrike is a c.1980 game by John Lee, published at Octogo Games.

The game is played in the following board,

Each player has 24 pieces that are initially placed over the circles (and also at the two colored filled squares which are called the defence area).

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player either:
    • moves a single piece to an (orthogonally or diagonally) adjacent empty square
    • moves a line of 2+ pieces in the same way
    • from a line of pieces, it is possible to move a piece at the extreme of the line, to the other extreme; if that square is occupied by an enemy piece (that belongs to a smaller line), this piece is captured
  • Wins the player that occupies the enemy's defence area
