Swish and Morro

Date: 2026-08-14 20:46 | Tags: old games

Swish and Morro are two games by Christian Freeling presented in GAMES #53 in 1984.

As said in the magazine article, both games use the same 8x8 board, material and also most rules, but each game has a different way to capture (one uses Checkers-like captures, the other uses custodian captures) which leads to very different dynamics.
