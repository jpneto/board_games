Ten-Q

Date: 2029-09-14 06:17 | Tags: old games, moku

Ten-Q is a 1955 uncredited game, published at K.T.Games.

This is a moku game played on the following board:

Each player has four pieces.

Rules:

  • Initially, each player drops, in turn, their pieces on the board
  • While a winning shape is not made, each player moves a friendly piece to an adjacent empty cell
  • Wins the player that played last (ie, the one that made the winning shape)

These are the winning shapes, any 4 in-a-row or a diamond shape:

The diamond shape can be made even if the middle cell is occupied by a piece (of either color)

The game then has a scoring system (see official rules below), where players repeat matches until one reaches 100 points.

This seems like a strange game to publish. It is too easy for the first player to force a win, so the only thing that might be of interest is how fast can each player reach the goal of 100 points.
