La Forteresse

Date: 2026-11-20 18:36 | Tags: old games

La Forteresse is a 1978 uncredited game, published at Nathan.

The game is played in the following board and setup:

La Forteresse has pieces moving in tandem over a straight line. This type of movement is reminiscent of Epaminondas, a game from 1975, being both precursors of Abalone (1987). In this game, the moving range is given by the piece at the end of the row of pieces. The goal is to reach the opponent's home base. Complete rules can be found here.
