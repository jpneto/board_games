Madam X

Date: 2029-04-06 16:05 | Tags: checkers, new games

Madam X is a 2004 game by Maurizio Tarrano, published at Faro.

This is a Checkers variant with the novelty of a changing board. Played on a 8x8 board, there are 32 black tiles, each with a small handle to help players pick it:

The tiles are placed in a checkered pattern and the twelve pieces each player has, are placed over the tiles, in the first three rows, just like Checkers:


initial setup

In the next rules, moves and jumps can be orthogonal or diagonal.

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player must:
    • move a friendly piece forwards or sideways to an empty black tile
    • or jump over an enemy piece, landing on the immediate next square, that must have an unoccupied black tile; the enemy piece is then captured
      • captures take precedence over moves
      • captures are mandatory; the piece may continue jumping if more captures are available (the jumping sequence can change direction between consecutive jumps)
    • after the move/jump, the player can optionally move a black tile to any empty square (an x-move)
      • if the player made a move, the tile is the one the moving piece just left
      • if the player made a jump, the tile is one of those previously occupied by the captured pieces
  • Promotion: a piece reaching the last row is promoted to King (a double-madam)
    • Kings can also move and capture backwards, and can slide over a straight line of black tiles
    • Kings can only be captured by other Kings
  • Wins the player that stalemates the adversary
