Blox-O

Date: 2029-10-18 23:55 | Tags: older games, moku

Blox-O is a 1923 uncredited game, published at Lubbers & Bell.

The game is a variant of the traditional Nine Men's Morris.

With every new 3 in-a-row, the player adds a blocking pin to the painted circle with the player's color, on that row (check the previous image). This prevents the player to repeat the pattern in two turns (one of the ploblems with the original game). So, each row allows for one, and only one, 3 in-a-row for each player.

When a player is reduced to less than three pieces, he loses the game.
