Virallo

Date: 2029-02-09 18:09 | Tags: flip pieces, new games

Virallo is a 1994 game by Patrick Carpentier.

The game is played on a 7x7 board.

There are 49 pieces Othello-like pieces. There are also 21 neutral pieces.

Adjacency is orthogonally or diagonally.

Rules:

  • Initially:
    • players take turns placing neutral pieces on the board (not in the corners)
      • however, these pieces must be arranged symmetrically along the board’s middle row
      • players do not need to place all neutral pieces
    • then, they place one piece per corner, same color on opposite colors

a valid setup
  • On his turn, a player either:
    • drops a friendly piece -- i.e., with his color facing up -- adjacent to another friendly piece
      • all adjacent enemy pieces are flipped over
    • passes his turn if there are no valid moves
  • When there are no more valid moves, the game ends and wins the player with more pieces

The symmetry rule is trying to provide a balanced initial position to reduce first player advantage. But I suspect that, if players could take turns placing pieces without restrictions, it would make for a better game. Another possibility would be for one player to build a setup, and let the other decide who starts (that could open the game to traps, creating a position with a initial good move that is hard to see).
