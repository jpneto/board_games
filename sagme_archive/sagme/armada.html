Armada

Date: 2027-05-28 14:25 | Tags: old games

Armada is a 1973 game by Sicco von Hülst, published at Pelikan.

The game is played in the following five hexagonal board (without the leftmost and rightmost hexes),

Each player has:

  • three big pieces (the galleons), initially placed on the double circles at the intersections
  • five medium pieces (the galleys), initially placed inside the first row of hexes
  • seven small pieces (the galleasses), initially placed on the single circles at the intersections

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player either:
    • moves a galleon up to three empty intersections
    • moves a galley to an adjacent empty hexagon
    • moves a gelleass up to two empty intersections
    • no jumps over pieces are allowed, 
  • A piece, after its move, can produce captures:
    • a galleon sinks a galleass if it moves to a corner adjacent to the galleass
    • a galley sinks a galleon if it moves to a hexagon with the galleon on a corner
    • a galleass sinks a galley if it moves to the corner of a hexagon where the galley is
    • if there are several capturing options, the player must choose which piece to capture
  • Wins the player that stalemates the adversary
    • if both players can still move, but are unable of further captures, wins the player with highest fleet score (galleons are worth 3 points, galleys 2 points, and galeasses 1 point)
