Blokado

Date: 2027-03-19 15:36 | Tags: old games

Blokado is a 1954 uncredited game, published at Diset.

Each player has four pieces of each one of his two colors. One player is Red and Green, the other is Yellow and Blue.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player makes two movements, twice with one piece or once with two friendly pieces
    • A piece can move orthogonally or diagonally to an empty position
    • If a piece lands on a position of the same color, it cannot move further
    • It the player only has one movable piece, this piece can only move once per turn
  • Wins the player that places his eight pieces on their corresponding positions of the same color
    • If both players are unable to achieve it, the game is a draw
