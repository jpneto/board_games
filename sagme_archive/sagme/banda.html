Banda

Date: 2028-08-25 08:49 | Tags: old games, tile games

Banda is a 1973 game by Alex Randolph, published at Otto Maier Verlag .


The game is played on a 5x5 board initially empty, and there are 36 tiles available,

Rules:

  • Initially, the first player chooses and drops two tiles anywhere, but they must not be adjacent
  • On her turn, the player either:
    • picks one available tile and drops it on an empty square, adjacent to another tile already on board, and matching their edges
    • challenges the position by declaring it is no longer possible to close a loop using all pieces
      • then, the adversary tries to disprove the assertion placing as many tiles as she wants [not clear if it is possible to take back these tiles, I would suggest no]
  • Wins the player that closes the loop or, in the case of a challenge, the player that is proven right

The challenge rule is very elegant, otherwise trying to play in a way that guarantees one loop is possible, would make it hard to determine if any move was legal or illegal. This is mutator that should be possible to apply successfully in other games.

The game comes with a 6x6 board that is used for the puzzle: build a loop using all 36 tiles.
