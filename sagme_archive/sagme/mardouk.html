Mardouk

Date: 2029-05-25 20:09 | Tags: new games, stack pieces, connection

Mardouk is a 2004 game by Vicent Everaert.

The game is played on a 9x9 board, initially empty.

There are 36 shared stackable pieces starting on reserve.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player either:
    • drops a piece on an empty square
    • if the adversary threatens to win in the next turn, the player may collect a row of three or more pieces in the same line (orthogonal or diagonal) and stack them on one of the row extremes
      • it is illegal to create a stack that cannot be distributed later (see next rule)
    • distributed an existing stack, placing one piece per square along a straight line 
      • those squares might be occupied with other pieces, that will get stacked
      • any square, after the distribution, that has exactly two pieces, has those pieces captured
      • the stack distribution is only valid if, at least, one capture occurs 
      • the stack distribution is only valid if no piece would go out of the board
  • Wins the player that connects his two opposite edges
    • if a player connects his two edges and also the opponent's two edges, at the same time, he loses the game
