Vega

Date: 2028-03-10 14:32 | Tags: game system, new games

Vega is a 1993 game by Christian Raffeiner and Helmut Walch, published at Peri Spiele.

The game is played on a triangular grid:

 initial setup for Battle Vega

Each player has ten tetrahedrons.

The game provides several different games, from one to four players. For two players there are Lyra, a Chinese Checkers variant, Ligne-Vega,a moku game, and Bataille-Vega, a game of capture. All games have the pieces starting on the board, and are based on moving pieces to the next adjacent triangles. More information at L’Escale à Jeux.
