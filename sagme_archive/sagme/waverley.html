Waverley

Date: 2027-02-15 09:48 | Tags: old games, connection

Waverley is a 1982 game by Chris C. Quinlan, Peter Wild, published at In Games.

It is played on the intersections of a 11x11 board, 


each player has 19 pieces plus 5 markers

Waverley is a connection game with a placement phase followed by a movement phase. When a diamond shape is made, the player uses a friendly marker, making that intersection his own. This is an interesting and novel way to approach orthogonal connection games.
