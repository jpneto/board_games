The Power of Two

Date: 2030-06-07 07:29 | Tags: flip pieces, new games

The Power of Two is a 1998 game by Arthur Blumberg, published at Kadon Enterprises.

The game is played on the following board:


the game's setup
the board has 64 intersections between several triangles

Each player has sixteen pieces, twelve of them start off-board. The pieces have two different sides: one represents a charged state, the other represents a drained state.

Definition

  • A doorway is open when a moving piece is placed adjacent to another piece (of either color)

Rules

  • On her turn, the player must perform the following sequence of moves:
    • Slide a friendly piece over a line of empty cells
    • If one or more doorways are open, each doorway implies that a new friendly piece must be dropped in an empty intersection that makes a triangle with the two respective doorway pieces
      • the new piece is placed in its charged state
      • if it is not possible to drop a piece, the move is invalid
    • All friendly pieces that created the doorway must drain their power: a charged piece is flipped and becomes drained, a drained piece is removed from the board and is returned to the player's reserve
  •  Wins the player that places all her stones on board

I could not find what happens if a player cannot make new doorways because the board is already quite full (there are a total of 32 pieces to be placed on 64 intersections). It would make sense to include a winning by stalemate.
