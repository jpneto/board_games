Tor

Date: 2029-06-29 21:58 | Tags: new games, tile games

Tor is a 1996 game by Alain Couchot and Bernard Klein.

The game is played on a 6x8 board, with the following setup:

Each player has two pieces, and there are 48 neutral tiles of four color (12 tiles per color).

Definitions:

  • a square is available if there one or two tiles on it, and is not occupied by a piece (a square cannot have more than two tiles)
  • a color is free for a player if their opponent do not have a piece directly on top of that color

Rules:

  • Initially, players take turns placing their pawns on a tile with a free color
  • Afterwards, each player on his turn must do the following three steps in sequence:
    • first: move a friendly piece to an orthogonally or diagonally adjacent available square with a free color
    • second: take the top tile from the square the piece moved from, and give it to the opponent
    • third: place the tile given previously by his opponent (except in the first turn, of course)
      • the tile must be placed on an available square with just one tile
      • a square cannot have two tiles with the same color
  • Wins the player that stalemates his opponent
