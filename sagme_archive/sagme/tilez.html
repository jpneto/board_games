Tilez!

Date: 2030-03-22 15:40 | Tags: old games, tile games

Tilez! is a 1997 game by Andy and Elliot Daniel, published at Enginuity.

The game is played in a 17x17 board initially empty,

There are 80 L-shaped pieces of five colors, red, yellow, blue, green, and black.

The original rules make players draw tiles from an hidden bag, and players hide their tiles. So the game does not have perfect information. However, it's possible to alter that without critically changing the game dynamics. One option is to give each player 40 pieces at the beginning, then one player divides into two sets, the other chooses, and the player that made the partition begins.

Rules

  • On his turn, the player must place a piece over empty squares, such that at least one unit square from the piece matches color with another unit square already on board (either a colored square, or a square from another piece).
    • pieces cannot be placed over the colored board squares 
    • pieces can can occupy starred squares
    • unit squares cannot be adjacent to different colored unit squares
      • however, if an empty square already has two or three neighbors of different colors, the player can play there, if it matches one of them
      • black matches all colors
  • Scoring: each placed tile earns one point for each one of its edges that match color with an adjacent unit square (notice that each piece has eight edges)
    • if the piece covers a star, its scoring doubles
    • if a piece get three or more points, that player has an extra turn
  • When the next player does not have a valid move, the game ends, and the player with the highest score wins.
