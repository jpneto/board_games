Selva

Date: 2029-06-22 13:15 | Tags: new games

Selva is a 2005 by Daniel Solis.

The game is played on a 13x13 board with the following setup:


Each player has ten regular pieces, and a royal piece (the tiger).

Here are the official rules. The rules include some variants, even adding new animal pieces. Btw, 'Selva' means jungle in Spanish and Portuguese.

The author remarked: This was an experiment in creating an abstracted simulation of billiard balls bumping into each other in chain reactions.

The game includes one interesting and original idea, the royal piece has a zone of influence, capturing any enemy piece that moves by it (well, tigers eat a lot...).
