Honeycomb

Date: 2027-05-07 11:41 | Tags: old games, wall pieces

Honeycomb is a 1970 game by W. B. Pink, published by Clipper and Marx Toys.

The game is played on the following hexagonal board, where it is possible to place walls between hexes,

Rules:

  • On his turn, a player drops a wall piece into an empty slot that is adjacent to another wall (except for the first move)
  • When a hexagon (a honey-cell) is closed, the current player marks it as one of his own
    • It is mandatory to close a honey-cell
    • The player, after closing a honey-cell, moves again
  • Wins the player with more honey-cells
