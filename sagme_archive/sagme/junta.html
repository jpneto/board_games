Junta

Date: 2027-09-03 10:09 | Tags: old games

Junta is a 1975 uncredited game, published by Capri and Spear's Games.

The game is played in the following board,

Each player has twelve regular pieces (the generals). The rectangular shape made of twelve circles in opposite corners (top-left and bottom-right) are the player's cities, and that's where the pieces start. The piece at each city corner becomes a royal piece (the president). Areas labeled with the two swords are called battle squares.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player moves one friendly general, which slides over a line of empty circles (no jumps over pieces are allowed)
    • The president never moves
  • There are two ways of capture; if after the move:
    • an enemy piece becomes trapped, i.e., having no liberties to move (this is called mobbing)
    • the piece occupies the fourth square around a battle square, he can capture one enemy piece at the corners of that battle square (this is called squaring) which is adjacent to a friendly piece also at the battle square (it does not need to be the current moving piece)
  • It is only allowed to capture one piece in a single turn (mobbing takes precedence over squaring)
    • A president can only captured by mobbing
  • Wins the player that occupies the adversary's city corner
    • If both players don't have enough strength to capture the other player's president, the game is a draw
