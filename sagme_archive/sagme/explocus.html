Explocus

Date: 2029-04-20 22:21 | Tags: old games, stack pieces

Explocus is a 1980s game by Martin Medema, published at MindSports.

The game is played on a 6x6 board, with the following setup:

Each player has twelve stackable pieces.

A stack has a maximum size of two at the corners, three at the edges, of four otherwise.

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player moves a friendly stack
    • a stack, or part of a stack, of N pieces, will move exactly N squares in a straight orthogonal line (the intermediate squares can be occupied by other pieces)
    • if the moving stack lands on an enemy stack, those enemy pieces change color
    • if the resulting stack becomes equal or higher than the stack's maximum size, the stack explodes, placing one piece per adjacent square (the remaining pieces, if any, stay at the original square)
      • a chain reaction can occur; if several stacks reach maximum size, the player decides the explosion sequence
  • Wins the player that captures all enemy pieces
