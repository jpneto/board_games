Billabong

Date: 2029-11-23 08:20 | Tags: checkers, new games

Billabong, aka Australian Checkers, is a 1992 game by Eric Solomon, published at Franjos Spieleverlag.

The game is played in a 14x16 board, with a 2x4 central area (the billabong), and a starting line (the stream):

Each player has five pieces (the kangaroos).

Definition:

  • A kangaroo can jump over another kangaroo of either color (called the pivot) if they share the same row, column or diagonal
    • The jumped distance must be equal before and after the pivot
    • The kangaroo must land on an empty square
    • No other kangaroos, besides the pivot, can be jumped over
    • A jump cannot cross over the billabong (it's valid to cross its four corners with a diagonal jump)
    • Jumps can be multiple
    • When the kangaroo starts jumping, a neutral black kangaroo (the referee) is positioned in its place (which can also be jumped over, during the jump sequence)

Rules:

  • Initially players, in alternate turns, place their kangaroos behind the stream
  • On his turn, the player either:
    • moves a friendly kangaroo to an orthogonal or diagonal adjacent empty square
    • jumps with a friendly kangaroo
  • When a kangaroo does a complete turn, and again crosses the stream, it is removed from play and placed in the billabong
  • Wins the player that places all his kangaroos in the billabong

There is a proposed variant that uses the non-playing kangaroos (the game comes prepared for four players) as cactus to be placed randomly, which then can be used to help players jump further.
