Barrier

Date: 2027-11-12 15:52 | Tags: rotating pieces, checkers, old games

Barrier is a 1975 game by Edward Schifman, published at Aladdin.

The game is played on a 8x8 board, where each player has 12 pieces.


the initial Checkers-like setup

Pieces move forward or diagonally forward, capture by jumping over, and are promoted at the last row, just like Draughts. The main difference is the middle two rows with the rotating diamonds that determine the direction of movement and act as a kind of barrier (thus the game's name).
