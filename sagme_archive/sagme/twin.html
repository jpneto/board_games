Twin

Date: 2028-09-22 17:34 | Tags: old games, stack pieces

Twin, aka Twill, is a 1975 game E. M. Rich, published at Pelikan.

The board has size 7x7. Each square has a central peg where pieces can be placed and stacked,

Each player has 25 tiles of size 1x2.

Rules:

  • On the first three turns, player take turns placing one piece over two empty squares (so these six tiles don't cover each other)
  • Afterwards, on his turn, the player drops a friendly piece on two board pegs
    • a piece cannot cover completely a previous uncovered piece (of either color), only half of it
    • a piece can be stacked on top of others
    • no more than two pieces can be stacked together
  • Wins the player with more complete uncovered tiles
