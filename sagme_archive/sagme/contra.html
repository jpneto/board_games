Contra

Date: 2027-12-17 07:31 | Tags: old games

Contra is a 1975 game by J. Brian McCarthy, published at Clipper.

The board is 8x8 with four extra squares at each player's side (the home bases),

Each player has eight arrows (two arrows per each one of four colors), and four pegs.

The colors are white, yellow, red, and blue. There is a cyclic order of strength that defines who can capture who: Red > White > Blue > Yellow > Red

Rules:

  • Initially, each player places their eight arrows in their first row, and the opponent's pegs in their own home bases
  • On his turn, the player moves a friendly arrow to an (orthogonally or diagonally) adjacent empty square
    • If the arrow then becomes adjacent to an enemy arrow, and their strengths are comparable, the weaker one must retreat two squares in the same direction of the adjacency (e.g., if they are side by side, the retreat is sideways)
    • If the retreat cannot be done (there's a piece in between, or the board's edge) the retreating arrow is captured and removed from the board 
    • After the retreat, a new conflict might occur resulting in a second retreat (and so on)
  • The goal is to reach the player's own pegs on the other side of the board.
    • An arrow that reaches the peg of the same color is worth 2 points
    • An arrow that reaches a peg of another color is worth 1 point 
      • [it is assumed that arrows at the enemy's home base can no longer move]
    • Wins the player with more points
      • [it is not said when the game ends; perhaps when one player reaches the fourth peg, or by mutual agreement because the position is blocked for both]
