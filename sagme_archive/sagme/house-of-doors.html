House of Doors

Date: 2029-04-13 19:20 | Tags: wall pieces, new games

House of Doors is 2004 game by Randy Cox.

The game is played in a 4x4 board with the following setup:

Each player has one piece, and there are eight shared wall/doors pieces.

Regarding doors:

  • A door in-between two squares breaks their adjacency.
  • A door turn is a 90º movement to a near-by and available edge (i.e., without a door). A valid door turn is allowed to push a piece on the current square to the next square (in the orthogonal direction of the swinging movement) if that new square is empty

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player makes two actions:
    • First, he moves his piece either by
      • moving it to an orthogonal adjacent empty square
      • turning a door such that his piece is pushed
    • Second, either the player
      • moves his piece (again) to an orthogonal adjacent empty square
      • turns any door, except the (eventual) door turned in the first action
  • Wins the player that stalemates the adversary
