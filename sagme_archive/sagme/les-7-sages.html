Les 7 Sages

Date: 2027-06-18 07:43 | Tags: old games

Les 7 Sages is a 1981 game by Max Gerchambeau, published at  Jeux Robert Laffont.

The game is played on a 15x15 board,

The external squares are the barbarian lands (there's a white line defining the frontier), the rest of the board is the kingdom. Also, the middle five squares on the player's first row are the player's city.

Each player has seven pieces (the sages) and one shared piece (the fool).

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player move one of his sages
    • A sage moves like the Chess Queen, and can move over all the board, except:
      • they can only move backwards vertically, except when chasing the fool (see below)
      • they cannot stop at the opponent's city (but they can slide over it)
    • A sage cannot slide over an occupied square and cannot end its movement in a square occupied by another sage (there are no captures)
    • A player cannot position three of their sages side by side
    • A sage can stop at the square where the fool is; then the fool is 'chased' and moves in a straight line in the direction it was chased (cf next rule)
  • When the fool is chased:
    • The fool cannot enter the barbarian lands
    • The fool stops on the first unoccupied square of its path 
    • If the chased fool encounters a sage, it is reflected 90º (either to the left or right) or 180º and continues moving
    • After the first reflection, the fool can stop anywhere on its path; further reflections during the same movement are optional
    • It is not possible for sages to reflect the fool into their own city
  • Each player can have one, and only one, sage on their city
  • Wins the player that moves the fool inside the opponent's city

There is a ZRF to play the game with Zillions.
