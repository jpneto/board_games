Karo

Date: 2029-03-16 13:43 | Tags: game system, new games

Karo is a 2005 game by Steffen Mühlhäuser, published at Steffen Spiele.

In fact, Karo is presented as a game system. There are eight pieces per player and 24 tiles (used to define boards). It comes with three different rulesets.

The first game is a moku, where players have the ability to change board squares:


 The second game is a Halma-like race game:


 The third is a race game where players remove their own pieces from the board a quickly as possible:


 The game includes a challenge for users to submit their own games based on Karo.

 In a later edition, a new game was added: Enlightenment.

Also, Lukas Köb in 2006 proposed a new game called Walpurgisnacht (Walpurgis Night is a pagan festival):

  • The 16 plates are placed in the circle as they touch each other. The red and white tiles are down with the circle, alternately distributed with an empty plate distance on the circle.
  • The players move a piece of any color on the circle. It could not move all the stones of a player just clockwise, the other player only counterclockwise. Pull the player the piece on a field that already has is a stone, is beaten and this is removed. Even the stones of the same color throw in this way from the game.
  • Stones with a circle drawn further and further down to a plate. Stones with the circle continues to move up two plates. After each train, the stone is turned over.
  • If a record is no token on in, or in the immediate neighborhood, it is removed from the circle. The circle is thus smaller. Example: With three empty plates are removed the mean side by side. With four empty plates, the middle two.
  • Who will be the last stone of their color on the plates in a circle, wins the game.
