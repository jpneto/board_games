Rock, Scissors, Paper

Date: 2029-12-07 16:21 | Tags: chess, new games

Rock, Scissors, Paper is a 2001 game by Greg Lam, published at  Pair-of-Dice Games.

As the name presupposes, each type of piece can capture a second type and be captured by the third type. There is a knock-out mechanism, increasing the tactical possibilities. This is a capture-the-flag game, which makes it also a Chess variant.
