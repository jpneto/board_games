OP Position

Date: 2030-09-06 22:15 | Tags: dice, new games

OP Position is 2004 uncredited game, published by Pyromyth Games.

The game is played on a 8x8 board, with the following setup:

setup: the dice has the four side towards North

There are eight pieces of two colors (Red and Black). There are also one die and 24 markers.

Rules

  • On his turn, the player move one red piece clockwise, or a black piece counter-clockwise
    • Pieces move the amount shown by the die's face up
    • A piece may not capture a piece of its own color. Captures are by replacement
    • A piece may only pass over a piece of its own color if a capture is made
    • If a capture is made, the player may but is not obligated to move the same piece as long as it captures another piece. 
  • After the piece's movement, the player tumbles the die to an orthogonal adjacent empty intersection
    • The die can only move in the central 5x5 area
    • A marker is placed on the die's previous intersection
  • If the player cannot move a piece, he instead removes a marker from the central area
  • Wins the player that captures four pieces of one color, or a total of six pieces
