Watubi

Date: 2029-12-21 14:22 | Tags: new games

Watubi is a 2002 game by Jürgen Reiche, published at Siebenstein Spiele.

The game is played on a hexagonal board with the following setup:


the original board does not have the left-most and right-most hexes,
but it seems an ad-hoc design decision to make a smaller game box

Each player has 13 pieces. Each piece has one side marked and the other unmarked.

Rules

  • On her turn, the player either:
    • moves a friendly piece to an adjacent empty hex
    • jumps over an adjacent piece, of either color, landing on the immediate hex that must be empty
      • jumps can be multiple, but it's illegal to pass through the same hex twice
      • every jumped piece is flipped 
      • the jumping piece is not flipped
  •  Wins the player that first makes a group of seven friendly pieces with all their faces up.

This is a game that takes the jumping mechanism from Halma, mixes it with Othello, and provides a goal from Lines of Action. A hybrid game, but an interesting one.
