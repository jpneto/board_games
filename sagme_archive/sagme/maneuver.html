Maneuver

Date: 2026-07-14 17:15 | Tags: old games

Maneuver is a 1978 uncredited game, published at Mattel.

This is an unification game, where the player's stones must be all in lines of three or more friendly stones. It has a random setup, but the game is still combinatorial.
