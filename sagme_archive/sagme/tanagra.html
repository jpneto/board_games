Tanagra

Date: 2028-02-18 09:51 | Tags: boardless, new games

Tanagra is a 1990 game by Gwenael Beuchet, published at Keit Vimp Bev.

The game does not have a board; players build one using triangular tiles:


There are 24 triangular tiles, six small and six large yellow pieces, and also six small and six large black pieces.

Rules (based on L’Escale à Jeux's):

  • One player takes the yellow pieces, the other takes the black pieces
  • Initially, players take turns dropping a triangle (at least one small triangle must be adjacent to another already in place) and a piece at a center of that triangle, until all pieces have been placed
  • Then, the player either:
    • moves a friendly piece to an edge-adjacent position
    • uses a friendly piece to jump over one or more identical pieces (color and shape)
    • captures are by replacement (not mandatory, nor multiple)
  • After moving his piece, if the original triangle is empty, the player can:
    • leave the triangle as it is
    • move the triangle to another place where at least one of its small triangles must adjacent to another
    • remove the triangle from play
  • Wins the player that captures all enemy pieces of a given size (small or large)

There are extra material and rules for more players. But, as usual, the blog focuses on two player games.
