Metis

Date: 2027-10-22 12:07 | Tags: dice, new games

Metis is a 2001 game by Benjamin Corliss, published at Allumbra.

The game is played on a 10x10 board with the following setup:

The dark and light squares are the players' temple.

Each player has 16 dice, and the top face of each die defines its strength.

Rules:

  • On his turn, a player moves one of his pieces orthogonally or diagonally, in a straight line, exactly the number of its strength
    • Pieces can jump over other pieces and they interact:
      • if a friendly die is jumped, one unit of strength is transferred to the jumped piece
      • if an enemy die is jumped, one unit of strength is transferred from the jumped piece
    • If piece falls below strength 1, it is removed from the board
    • If piece gains more than strength 6, it is also removed from the board
  • Capture is by replacement, but only if the attacker's strength is higher than the attacked's
    • Each capture increases the attacker's strength by one unit
    • Players can capture their own pieces
  • Wins the player that occupies the enemy's palace earning 8 points:
    • Each dark-gray filled square (the inner chamber) values 2 points
    • Each light-gray filled square (the outer chamber) values 1 points
  • The winner's score is the number of seats he captured minus the number of seats captured by the adversary

Note that an adversary reduced to three or less pieces immediately loses, since it is impossible to score 8 points.

The original rules provide some strategy tips:

  • Position pieces in the center of the board where they have a better chance of exchanging power with your other pieces.
  • This also gives you a greater opportunity to block an aggressive move by your opponent.
  • If you have the chance, position a (6) in your own temple. A (6) is very difficult to dislodge from power.
  • Do not overlook moves in which you would cannibalize your own priests. Sometimes these moves are your best alternative.
  • Do not accumulate too many high value pieces as this will lessen your mobility.
