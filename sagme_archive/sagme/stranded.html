Stranded

Date: 2027-06-11 07:04 | Tags: old games, wall pieces

Stranded is a 1981 game by Philip O'Neill, published by Spear's Games.

The game is played in a hexagonal board, where edges allow for the placement of wall pieces (the roads):

Initially the board starts with all roads already in place. Each player's piece starts on the middle position at opposite board edges.

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player moves her piece in a straight line over a sequence of roads; then the first used road in the piece's path is removed from the board
    • however, the piece cannot stop at a position within moving range of the opponent's piece (i.e., to be an invalid move, there must be a line of roads connecting both positions)
  • Wins the player that stalemates the opponent

This is a game with very simple rules, but not necessarily a simple game. It does not take long to play, the number of moves is limited by the number of roads, which makes for very tactical matches. Its game tree might be simple enough for a computer to search it completely.
