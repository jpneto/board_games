Attention!

Date: 2027-05-21 12:45 | Tags: older games

Attention! is a 1897 game by Otto Sterkel, published at Otto Maier Verlag.

The game is played in this board made of concentric circles,

Player Color has the 30 pieces (10 of each color) and player Black owns the 18 black pieces placed at the center:

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player moves a friendly piece like a Checkers-piece, moving to an adjacent empty circle, or capture by jumping over an enemy piece
    • Sideways moves/captures around the circles are also valid
    • Colored pieces can only capture black pieces
    • Colored pieces cannot move/capture backwards, but black pieces can
    • Captures are not mandatory 
  • The move sequence is as follows:
    • Initially, Color moves two pieces per color and then Black moves one piece per each one of the triangular areas
    • For the remaining turns:
      • Color moves one piece per color
      • Black moves as many pieces as there are colors left
  • Black wins by capturing all colored pieces, while the colored pieces win if they occupy the central three circles [note: perhaps just one central circle is needed to win].

The colored pieces can be used by more players, making it a game for 2 to 4 players. The game, inspired by Halma and Checkers, has patent GB189600312.
