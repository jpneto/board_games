Captures

Date: 2026-10-27 15:13 | Tags: old games, rotating pieces

Captures (aka Jagdfieber) is a 1972 uncredited game, published at MB Jeux and MB Spiele.

The game can be played by 2, 3, or 4 players.

Each player has three leopards and one trap-box. This is the board:

 

In the setup, the trap-boxes are placed on the board’s squares, arranged so that the openings of the boxes face upward. The three leopards are placed on the three fields marked with circles of the same color:

Rules

  • On his turn, each player either:
    • move his trap-box to an adjacent orthogonal position, without rotating it.
    • tilt the box to an adjacent position if it captures an enemy leopard (so, the box totally encloses the leopard piece).
  • If the trap-box reaches a cyan position, the player can, in his next move, move instead one of his remaining leopards to an adjacent empty position
    • After a leopard moves, the box must arrive on a new cyan position, for any friendly leopard to move again
  • If the trap-box moves to a circle position, it can rotate 90º left, right, up or down
  • The game ends when one player has captured all of another player’s leopards.
    • For the multi-player game:
      • Each captured leopard counts as six points. 
      • The winner is the player who has scored the most points at the end of the game.
