Archimedes

Date: 2028-02-11 09:19 | Tags: dice, new games, 3 players

Archimedes is a 1995 game by Niek Neuwahl, published at Editrice Giochi.

The game is played in the following board with triangular tiling:


initial setup

The pieces are dice-shaped and fit into the board's triangles. Each player has four pieces.

Rules:

  • On his turn, the player moves one of his pieces
    • A piece moves exactly as many positions as the number of all pieces, friend or foe, that are in the three lines that intersect at its position (similar to Lines of Action, but here there are no captures)
      • The only exception is if the piece reaches the last row, there it can stop before moving all the remaining required steps
    • Pieces cannot move backwards
    • Pieces cannot be adjacent, not even during a piece's movement (so, each piece will typically block three positions, or two positions if it is at the edge)
    • If the player cannot move, he passes his turn
  • Wins the player that moves all friendly pieces to his last row
