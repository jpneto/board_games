Le Trioker

Date: 2027-10-29 13:58 | Tags: dominoes, old games, game system

Le Trioker is a 1972 game by Marc Odier, published at Jeux Robert Laffont.

This is a game that comes with a set of triangular dominoes:

and includes a two player game, a bidding game, and also proposes some puzzles. In fact, this material can be seen, as any set of dominoes, as a game system.

The game used the following hexagonal board,

Rules:

  • On her turn, the player drops one of their triangles next to an already placed triangle
    • Two adjacent vertices must have the same number of dots
    • A player who cannot place a triangle passes her turn
  • Wins the player who drops her last piece or, if the game is blocked, the one with the fewest remaining pieces

The L'Escale à jeux has a ruleset with more information, including the puzzles.
