Crosstrack

Date: 2027-03-15 11:22 | Tags: new games, connection

Crosstrack is a 1994 game by Philip L. Shoptaugh, published at Discovery Toys.

The game is played on the following octagonal board, 

There are octagonal pieces, and also smaller squared pieces to be played at the interstitial squares:


Each player picks octagonal pieces of two colors (the game can be played by four players). The small square pieces are shared. Players pick, on their turn, one available piece and place it on an empty cell, trying to connect their opposite sides. The tracks are shared, even if the piece colors are not.
